# Corvane Supply Planning Policy — Chapter 2: Safety Stock

Corvane policy CSP-02. Owner: Director of Supply Planning.

## 2.1 Standard safety stock
Safety stock is set in weeks of forward requirement:
- A-class components (castings, stators, encoders, gear sets, controller boards): **2 weeks**.
- B- and C-class components: **1.5 weeks**.
Safety stock units = safety-stock weeks x average weekly requirement (13-week forecast).

## 2.2 Triggered increase for an unreliable lane
When a supplier's on-time delivery for a component falls **below 80% for four consecutive weeks**, or a port or lane disruption with an estimated delay of **more than 14 days** is active on the lane, the planner raises safety stock for that component at the affected plant by **one week** (for example 2 to 3 weeks for an A-class component) until on-time delivery has been above 90% for eight consecutive weeks. The increase is a policy change approved by the plant manager (CSP-01 §1.4).

## 2.3 Cost of carrying safety stock
Annual carrying cost is 25% of the standard cost of the additional units (capital, storage, handling, obsolescence risk). Planners should quote this annual cost when proposing an increase.

## 2.4 Dipping into safety stock
Safety stock exists to be used. A projected dip below safety stock for one or two weeks on a reliable lane does not require a mitigation plan; a projected stock-out always does.
