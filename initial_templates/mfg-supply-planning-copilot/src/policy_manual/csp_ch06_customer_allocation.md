# Corvane Supply Planning Policy — Chapter 6: Customer Allocation During a Shortage

Corvane policy CSP-06. Owner: VP Sales Operations and VP Supply Chain.

## 6.1 Allocation is the last resort
Customer allocation (shipping less than ordered) is used only when transfers, alternate sources and premium freight cannot close a shortage in time (CSP-03 §3.1).

## 6.2 Allocation order
When finished units are short, allocate in this order:
1. Tier A strategic accounts (for example Atlas Warehouse Robotics, Northgate Packaging Systems, Orbital Semiconductor Tools, Brandt Intralogistik) — ship in full against the committed date.
2. Tier B accounts — ship at least 80% of the order, balance within 2 weeks.
3. Tier C accounts — ship on a fair-share basis.

## 6.3 Customer communication
Customer service must notify any customer whose order will ship late at least 5 business days before the requested ship date, with a recovery date. Planners should list the affected open orders (customer, quantity, requested ship date) in the mitigation plan.

## 6.4 Service targets
Corvane's customer-service target is a **97% unit fill rate** per plant per month and 95% line fill rate. Fill rate is units shipped divided by units ordered.
