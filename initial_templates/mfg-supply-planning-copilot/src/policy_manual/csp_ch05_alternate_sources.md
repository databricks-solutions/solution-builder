# Corvane Supply Planning Policy — Chapter 5: Alternate Sources and Dual Sourcing

Corvane policy CSP-05. Owner: Director of Strategic Sourcing.

## 5.1 Qualified alternates only
Only suppliers with a completed PPAP (production part approval) for the exact part number may be used as an alternate source. The sourcing table records the qualification status.

## 5.2 HX-220 gear housing
- Primary: **Nanhai Precision Castings** (Foshan, China), ocean via the Port of Yantian to Long Beach and rail to Dayton, contract lead time 56 days, USD 142 per piece.
- Qualified alternate: **Ridgeline Foundry** (Mansfield, Ohio). PPAP Level 3 approved in March 2026. USD 183 per piece, 21-day lead time, capacity up to 1,200 pieces per month, minimum order 400 pieces, one-time USD 6,500 pattern/setup charge on the first order.
- Share limit: Ridgeline may supply at most **40% of Dayton's monthly HX-220 volume during its first six months** as a new source.

## 5.3 When to activate an alternate
Activate the alternate when the primary lane has an active disruption with more than 14 days of delay, or the primary's on-time delivery is below 80% for four consecutive weeks. A dual-source order is also the recommended way to protect the weeks AFTER an immediate shortage has been covered.

## 5.4 EN-17 encoder
Kessler Sensorik is the sole qualified source for the EN-17 encoder. During an allocation, cover shortages by transferring encoders between Corvane plants (including obsolete encoders from the sunset SG-300 line at Monterrey) before considering customer allocation.
