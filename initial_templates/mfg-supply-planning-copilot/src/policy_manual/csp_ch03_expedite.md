# Corvane Supply Planning Policy — Chapter 3: Expediting and Premium Freight

Corvane policy CSP-03. Owner: VP Supply Chain.

## 3.1 Order of preference for covering a shortage
When a stock-out exposure is detected, planners evaluate mitigations in this order and use the cheapest combination that closes the shortage in time:
1. **Re-allocate inventory between Corvane plants** (inter-plant transfer), starting with excess and obsolete stock (CSP-04).
2. **Pull in or increase an order with a qualified alternate supplier** (CSP-05).
3. **Premium freight (air expedite)** from the primary supplier.
4. Customer allocation (CSP-06) — only when 1 to 3 cannot close the gap in time.

## 3.2 Premium freight approval
Premium (air) freight is the most expensive lever and is justified only when a cheaper lever cannot arrive before the first stock-out week. Premium freight above **USD 25,000** needs plant-manager approval and above **USD 50,000** needs VP Supply Chain approval (CSP-01 §1.4). The request must state the revenue at risk and why a transfer or alternate source cannot cover.

## 3.3 Supplier air offers
Air-expedite offers from suppliers (for example Nanhai Precision Castings' offer to fly part of an HX-220 lot at a per-piece premium) pull units forward from the NEXT production lot — the ocean quantity of that lot is reduced by the same amount. Planners must reflect this in the projection.
