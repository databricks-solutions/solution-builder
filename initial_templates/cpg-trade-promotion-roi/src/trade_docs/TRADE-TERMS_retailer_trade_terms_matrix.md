## Allowance funding
- Crestwood funds **50%** of the consumer discount for TPR, Feature + Display and BOGO events, and **40%** for Multi-buy events (the retailer co-funds the rest from its margin).
- Allowance per unit = regular shelf price × discount depth × Crestwood funding share.
- Off-invoice: the allowance is paid on every unit invoiced during the deal window (the week before the event + the event weeks). Scan-based: paid on units scanned at the register during the event weeks.

## Unit economics (Crestwood, per unit)
- List price to the retailer = 64% of the suggested shelf price; unit COGS = 20% of the suggested shelf price; unit contribution margin = list price − COGS.
- Example: Crestwood Kettle Sea Salt 8 oz — suggested shelf $3.99, list $2.55, COGS $0.80, contribution margin $1.75.
