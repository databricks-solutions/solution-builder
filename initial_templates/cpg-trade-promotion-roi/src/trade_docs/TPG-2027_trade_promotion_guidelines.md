# Crestwood Snacks Co. — Trade Promotion Guidelines FY2027 (TPG-2027)

Document owner: Revenue Growth Management (RGM) · Approved by: VP Sales North America, CFO · Effective: start of FY2027 · Applies to: every trade promotion on Crestwood Kettle, Crestwood Classic, Ridgeline Tortilla, Twistwell Pretzels and Crestwood Variety at every retail account.

## 1. Purpose
Trade spend is Crestwood's second-largest cost after COGS. These guidelines make every promotion earn its money: each event is planned against a forecast, evaluated after it runs (post-event analysis, PEA-2027) and re-planned when it does not pay back.

## 2. Roles
- **Key Account Manager (KAM)** proposes events in the account's promo calendar and negotiates with the retailer.
- **Revenue Growth Management (RGM)** owns the forecast, the post-event analysis and the approval of the quarterly calendar.
- **VP Sales** approves exceptions (§4.1) and any calendar where total forecast ROI is below target.

## 3. Return on investment
### 3.1 Definition
ROI = (incremental gross profit − trade spend) ÷ trade spend, measured against the ML baseline (see PEA-2027 §2).
- Incremental gross profit counts the promoted SKU's incremental units **after** the post-event dip (pantry loading) at the unit contribution margin (list price − COGS), **minus** the margin lost on cannibalized sister SKUs.
- Trade spend = the per-unit allowance paid (on scanned units for scan-based settlement, on every unit bought on deal for off-invoice settlement) + fixed fees (feature ad, display).
- An ROI below 0% means the event **lost money**: Crestwood would have earned more by not running it.

### 3.2 Hurdle and target
- **Hurdle: every planned event must forecast ROI ≥ 0%.** An event forecast below 0% must be re-planned before the calendar is locked, or carry a VP Sales exception with a written strategic rationale (e.g. a new-item launch).
- **Target: forecast ROI ≥ +15%.** Events between 0% and +15% are "below target" and are reviewed but may run.
- The quarterly calendar must forecast a blended ROI ≥ +15% per account.

## 4. Tactic guardrails
### 4.1 Discount depth
- Maximum consumer discount depth **30%** without an exception. Deeper events — including **BOGO (buy one get one free = 50% effective discount)** — require a VP Sales exception **and** a forecast ROI ≥ 0%.
- Promo price points must respect the retailer's price-ending rules.

### 4.2 Preferred tactics
- **Feature + Display with a 20-25% TPR** is the preferred volume-driving tactic: display and circular feature drive most of the lift, so the allowance per unit stays moderate.
- **Shelf TPR 15-20%** is the default maintenance tactic.
- **Multi-buy (2 for $X)** is allowed on impulse SKUs; watch pantry loading.
- **BOGO** is discouraged: it pays a 50% allowance on every unit that would have sold anyway (the "subsidised baseline"), and history shows the deepest pantry loading and cannibalization of any tactic.

### 4.3 Funding and settlement
- **Scan-based settlement is preferred**: Crestwood pays the allowance only on units the shopper actually buys during the event.
- Off-invoice allowances (paid on every case the retailer buys during the deal window) invite **forward buying** — the retailer buys extra at deal price and sells it after the event at full price. Where an account accepts scan-down settlement for a tactic, the plan must use it.

## 5. Re-planning a money-losing event
When a planned event forecasts ROI < 0%:
1. Keep the event slot (week, SKU) where the account's Joint Business Plan (JBP) requires a minimum number of events — **re-plan the tactic, do not delete the event**.
2. Choose the compliant alternative (§4) with the highest forecast net profit; prefer scan-based settlement.
3. Record the decision (original and new tactic, forecast net-profit change, volume change, approver) in the promo-plan system of record before the calendar is sent to the retailer.
4. Changes must reach the retailer at least 4 weeks before the event start (see the account's JBP for its own notice period).

## 6. Post-event analysis
Every completed event is evaluated within 6 weeks of its end (PEA-2027). Events that lost money are reviewed in the monthly RGM council; a tactic that loses money at an account twice in a row may not be planned again at that account without an exception.
