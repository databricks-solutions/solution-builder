# ValuMax Stores × Crestwood Snacks — Joint Business Plan FY2027 (JBP-VALUMAX-FY27)

Parties: ValuMax Stores (Mass channel, 1,850 stores, national) and Crestwood Snacks Co. · Category: Salty Snacks · Crestwood KAM: Tom Becker · ValuMax buyer: Salty Snacks category team · Term: FY2027.

## 1. Joint growth objectives
- Grow Crestwood salty-snack retail sales at ValuMax +4% vs last year while growing ValuMax category margin.
- Improve promotional efficiency: both parties agree that events must be profitable for both sides; the promo calendar is reviewed jointly each quarter using post-event analysis.

## 2. Promotional commitments (per quarter)
- **Minimum promoted events: Crestwood commits to at least 6 promoted events per quarter** across its Salty Snacks SKUs at ValuMax. The event count is a JBP commitment; **tactics and depths may be changed** within the notice period (§4), but events may not be removed without the buyer's agreement.
- **Endcap displays: at least 2 per quarter.** ValuMax offers Crestwood up to **8 endcap display slots per quarter** (display fee per the trade terms matrix).
- Circular feature: each event may carry a feature ad; feature fees per the trade terms matrix.

## 3. Funding and settlement
- ValuMax's default settlement is **off-invoice** (allowance deducted from the invoice for every case bought during the deal window).
- **Scan-based (scan-down) settlement is accepted for TPR and Feature + Display events** from FY2026 onward, at Crestwood's request. BOGO events remain off-invoice.
- Price points: promo prices end in .99, .49 or .00.

## 4. Changes to the calendar
- Changes of tactic, depth or settlement must be notified to the ValuMax buyer **at least 4 weeks before the event start**.
- A re-planned event keeps its slot (week and SKU) and counts toward the §2 minimums.

## 5. Review cadence
Quarterly business review with post-event results per event: volume lift, net incremental units, pantry loading, trade spend and ROI.
