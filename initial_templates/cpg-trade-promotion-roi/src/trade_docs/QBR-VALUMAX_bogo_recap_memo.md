# Memo — ValuMax Salty Snacks promotions recap (prepared for the ValuMax quarterly business review)

From: Tom Becker, Key Account Manager, ValuMax Stores · To: Sales leadership · Subject: why we keep the BOGO program

## Summary (KAM view)
- The ValuMax BOGO program is our biggest volume driver: the Crestwood Kettle Sea Salt 8 oz BOGO delivered more than three times normal weekly volume, and ValuMax asked us to repeat BOGOs every quarter.
- The buyer is happy with the BOGOs — they drive store traffic and circular impact — and they have started asking for the same on Crestwood Classic, the Family size and Ridgeline Tortilla.
- My recommendation for next quarter's draft calendar: keep four BOGO events (Kettle Sea Salt 8 oz, Classic Original, Kettle Family 13 oz, Ridgeline Restaurant-Style) and two endcap displays to meet the JBP.

## Open questions from Finance (not yet answered)
- Finance asked why ValuMax shipments during BOGO weeks are much higher than ValuMax's own POS sales, and why ValuMax orders drop for several weeks after each BOGO.
- Finance also asked why Crestwood Kettle Jalapeno and Honey BBQ sales at ValuMax fall during Kettle Sea Salt BOGO weeks.
- RGM has not yet run the post-event analysis on the BOGO events.
