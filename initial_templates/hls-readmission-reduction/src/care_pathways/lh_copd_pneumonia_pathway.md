# Larkspur Health — COPD and Pneumonia Care Transitions Pathway (CTP-PULM-02)

## COPD exacerbation
- Follow-up with the Pulmonary Clinic or PCP **within 7 days**; pulmonary rehabilitation referral **within 30 days** of discharge (reduces readmissions and mortality).
- Inhaler technique teach-back with the patient's own devices; confirm the discharge inhaler regimen is covered by the patient's plan (medication cost is a frequent cause of non-adherence).
- **New home oxygen**: the DME vendor must confirm delivery and setup **before** discharge, or the discharge is delayed. Hypoxia because oxygen was not set up at home is a preventable readmission.
- Written COPD action plan (green / yellow / red zones) with a rescue-pack decision documented.
- Smoking cessation counseling and referral if the patient smokes.
- Interpreter-supported teaching for patients whose preferred language is not English; written materials in the preferred language.

## Pneumonia
- Follow-up within 7-14 days; complete the antibiotic course as prescribed.
- Pneumococcal and influenza vaccination status reviewed and offered before discharge.
- High-risk patients (age ≥ 80, Charlson ≥ 5, lives alone) receive a 48-hour call.

## Home health criteria (pulmonary)
New home oxygen with a caregiver who has not been trained; ≥ 2 COPD admissions in 12 months; ambulatory saturation < 88% on room air; lives alone with limited mobility.
