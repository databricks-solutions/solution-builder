# Larkspur Health — Community Resources, Home Health & SNF Referral Guide (CT-305)

## Larkspur Ride Assist (transportation)
Free rides to Larkspur clinics, labs and pharmacies for patients within 30 miles who do not drive or lack a reliable ride. Book through the care-transitions coordinator at least 24 hours ahead (same-day for post-discharge visits within 72 hours). Wheelchair-accessible vehicles available. Covers up to 6 round trips in the 30 days after discharge.

## Meds-to-beds (bedside prescription delivery)
Discharge prescriptions filled by the outpatient pharmacy and delivered to the bedside before the patient leaves; the pharmacist completes the medication counseling. Live on Millbrook 2 West; available on request for Larkspur Regional units Monday-Friday until 5 pm. Financial counseling and copay assistance screening included.

## Larkspur Home Health
Skilled nursing, physical therapy and remote weight/vital monitoring. First visit within 48 hours for high-risk heart failure and COPD patients. Referral requires a face-to-face encounter documented by the discharging physician. Home health nurses can draw labs (BMP) at home.

## Skilled nursing facility (SNF) transitions
Send the discharge summary, medication list and pending results to the SNF before the patient arrives; the SNF physician sees the patient within 72 hours. Preferred SNF network partners report readmissions monthly.

## Telehealth follow-up
When no in-person 7-day slot is available (weekends, holidays), book a video or phone visit with the HF Transitions Clinic NP within 72 hours, followed by an in-person visit within 14 days.

## Outreach task types used by the care-transitions team
Book 7-day follow-up · 48-hour post-discharge call · Pharmacist medication reconciliation call · Lab order / draw (BMP) · Home health referral · Ride Assist booking · Financial counseling referral · Interpreter-supported teach-back.
