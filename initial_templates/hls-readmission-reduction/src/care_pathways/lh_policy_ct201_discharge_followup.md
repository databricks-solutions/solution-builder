# Larkspur Health Policy CT-201 — Discharge Follow-up Scheduling and Weekend Discharges

Effective: rev. 3 · Owner: VP Patient Care Services · Scope: all inpatient units, all three hospitals.

## 1. Policy
Every patient discharged with an HRRP condition (heart failure, AMI, pneumonia, COPD, CABG, elective hip/knee replacement) leaves the hospital with a **scheduled** follow-up appointment: within **7 days** for heart failure, AMI and COPD; within 14 days for the others. The appointment is booked by the unit, not delegated to the patient.

## 2. Roles
- **Discharge navigator (RN)** — books appointments, completes the transition checklist, makes the 48-hour call. Each cardiac and medicine unit has weekday navigator coverage; 4 West and 5 East have **weekend navigator coverage (Friday-Sunday)**.
- **Charge nurse** — owns the checklist when the navigator is not on shift.
- **Care-transitions coordinator** — works the risk-ranked worklist, reviews every high-risk patient before discharge and assigns outreach tasks.

## 3. Friday and weekend discharge protocol
3.1 Friday, Saturday and Sunday discharges have historically been the most likely to leave without a booked follow-up (clinic scheduling closed, fewer staff).
3.2 For a weekend discharge the unit books the follow-up in a **reserved "weekend discharge" slot** (HF Transitions Clinic holds Friday-afternoon booking slots and a Saturday session for this purpose) or, if none is available, schedules a **telehealth visit within 72 hours**.
3.3 If the weekend navigator position is vacant, the charge nurse must page the on-call care-transitions coordinator for every HRRP patient before discharge. The coordinator creates an outreach task for Monday 9 am at the latest: book the visit, confirm the medications, arrange transportation.
3.4 Staffing gaps that remove weekend navigator coverage or clinic slots for more than 2 weeks must be escalated to the Director of Care Transitions and reported to the Readmissions Committee.

## 4. The 48-hour call
Every high- and medium-risk patient receives a phone call within 48 hours of discharge (by the next business day for weekend discharges). The call confirms: prescriptions filled, understanding of medication changes, appointment date and transport, red-flag symptoms.

## 5. Monitoring
The Readmissions Committee reviews monthly: % of HRRP discharges with a follow-up booked before discharge (target ≥ 90%), % completed within 7 days, 30-day readmission rate by unit and by discharge weekday, and the estimated HRRP excess readmission ratio per condition.
