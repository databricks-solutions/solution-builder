# Westbrook Department of Human Services — Economic Assistance Policy Manual
## Chapter 4 — SNAP Income, Resources and Benefit Amount
Revision 2026-1 (federal fiscal year 2026 standards, effective October 1, 2025).

### §4.1 Gross income test (130% of the federal poverty level)
Households without an elderly or disabled member must have gross monthly income **at or below** 130% of the federal poverty level (7 CFR 273.9(a)(1)). Income exactly equal to the limit passes.

| Household size | Gross monthly limit (130% FPL) | Net monthly limit (100% FPL) | Maximum monthly allotment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1,696 | $1,305 | $298 |
| 2 | $2,292 | $1,763 | $546 |
| 3 | $2,888 | $2,221 | $785 |
| 4 | $3,483 | $2,680 | $994 |
| 5 | $4,079 | $3,138 | $1,183 |
| 6 | $4,675 | $3,596 | $1,421 |
| 7 | $5,271 | $4,055 | $1,571 |
| 8 | $5,867 | $4,513 | $1,789 |
| Each additional member | +$596 | +$459 | +$218 |

### §4.2 Converting pay to monthly income
Weekly pay × 4.3; biweekly pay × 2.15; semimonthly pay × 2; monthly pay × 1. Average the stubs covering the 30 days before filing.

### §4.3 Anticipated income and terminated income
Eligibility and benefits are based on income the household **reasonably anticipates** receiving in the certification period (7 CFR 273.10(c)(1)). Income from a job that has ended is not anticipated income once the final paycheck has been received. Example: an applicant laid off from the Tamarack Paper Company mill whose final paycheck arrived before the filing date has $0 anticipated earned income from that job, even though her last pay stub shows wages above the gross income limit. Use the layoff notice to verify the end of employment.

### §4.4 Resource limit
Countable liquid resources (cash, checking and savings) must not exceed **$3,000**, or **$4,500** if a household member is age 60 or older or disabled (7 CFR 273.8(b)). The household's statement of resources is accepted unless it is questionable; a bank statement is not required for every case.

### §4.5 Benefit calculation
Net income = gross income − 20% earned-income deduction − standard deduction − allowable shelter and dependent-care costs. Monthly benefit = maximum allotment − 30% of net income. One- and two-person eligible households receive at least $24. A household with $0 net income receives the maximum allotment for its size (for a household of 4: $994).
