# Westbrook Department of Human Services — Economic Assistance Policy Manual
## Chapter 1 — Civil Rights, Equity and Performance Monitoring
Revision 2026-1.

### §1.1 Nondiscrimination
Determinations are made only on program rules. Race, color, national origin, sex, age, disability and political beliefs are never used to decide eligibility (7 CFR 272.6). Race and ethnicity are collected on a voluntary basis for civil-rights reporting only.

### §1.2 Equity monitoring
The Division of Economic Assistance reviews monthly, by county and by demographic group:
- 30-day (SNAP), 45-day (Medicaid) and 7-day (expedited) timeliness;
- approval rate;
- **procedural denial rate** — denials for failure to provide verification or missed interviews. A high procedural denial rate means eligible residents are losing benefits over paperwork ("churn"). A county whose procedural denial rate is more than twice the statewide rate triggers a review under §2.7.

### §1.3 Geographic access
Rural residents are more likely to apply by mail and less likely to have broadband. Offices must offer phone interviews, accept documents by mail or photo upload, and use collateral contacts before denying a rural household for missing verification.

### §1.4 Use of AI tools
AI tools may read documents, check rules and draft notices to reduce caseworker effort. Every AI output is a recommendation: the caseworker reviews the evidence and makes the determination. Each AI-assisted case records which documents were read, which rules were checked, and who approved the decision.
