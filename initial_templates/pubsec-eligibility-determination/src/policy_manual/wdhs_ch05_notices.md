# Westbrook Department of Human Services — Economic Assistance Policy Manual
## Chapter 5 — Notices to Residents
Revision 2026-1.

### §5.1 Plain-language standard
Every notice must be written in plain language at or below an **8th-grade reading level**: short sentences, everyday words, active voice, and the most important information first. Do not use acronyms without explaining them. Address the resident by name.

### §5.2 Language access
Send notices in the household's preferred language (Title VI of the Civil Rights Act; 7 CFR 272.4(b)). Spanish notices are required for every household that prefers Spanish. For other languages, include the multilingual tagline sheet (DHS-0100) offering free interpretation.

### §5.3 Approval notice (DHS-1170) must include
1. The program and the decision (approved).
2. The monthly benefit amount and the certification period.
3. When and how benefits will arrive (EBT card mailed within 5 business days).
4. The household's duty to report changes (income over the gross limit) within 10 days.
5. The right to request a fair hearing if the household disagrees with the amount.

### §5.4 Denial notice (DHS-1171) must include
1. The decision (denied) and the **specific reason**, including the rule and its citation (for example: "Your household's gross monthly income of $3,310 is above the limit of $2,292 for a household of 2 — 7 CFR 273.9(a)(1)").
2. The right to a **fair hearing within 90 days** of the notice date (7 CFR 273.15(g); 42 CFR 431.221(d) for Medicaid), how to request one, and that free legal aid is available.
3. The right to reapply at any time.

### §5.5 Request for verification (DHS-1150) must include
1. A list of **every** missing item, each with examples of documents that will work.
2. The due date — at least 10 days from the notice date.
3. All the ways to submit: upload at benefits.westbrook.gov, mail, fax, or drop-off at the county office.
4. An offer of help: "If you cannot get these papers, call your caseworker — we can help."
5. A statement that the application stays open while the office waits.

### §5.6 Caseworker review
AI-drafted notices are drafts. A caseworker must review, edit if needed, and approve every notice before it is sent. The final decision is always made by the caseworker.
