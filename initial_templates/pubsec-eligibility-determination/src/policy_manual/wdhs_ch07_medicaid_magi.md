# Westbrook Department of Human Services — Economic Assistance Policy Manual
## Chapter 7 — Medicaid (MAGI Adult Group)
Revision 2026-1.

### §7.1 MAGI income limit (138% FPL)
Adults 19–64 qualify when household modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) is at or below **138% of the federal poverty level** (42 CFR 435.119; MAGI methodology 42 CFR 435.603).

| Household size | Monthly limit (138% FPL) |
|---|---|
| 1 | $1,800 |
| 2 | $2,433 |
| 3 | $3,065 |
| 4 | $3,698 |
| 5 | $4,330 |
| 6 | $4,963 |
| 7 | $5,595 |
| 8 | $6,228 |

### §7.2 Identity
Verify identity with a current photo ID or through electronic data sources (42 CFR 435.407).

### §7.3 Residency
The applicant must live in Westbrook with intent to remain (42 CFR 435.403). Verify with a lease, utility bill or other residency document, or electronically through state data sources when available (42 CFR 435.956(c)).

### §7.4 Citizenship and immigration status
Verify citizenship electronically through the federal data services hub (SSA match). When the electronic match succeeds, **no paper documents are needed**. When it fails, give the applicant a 90-day reasonable opportunity period to provide documents (42 CFR 435.406; 435.956(a)-(b)).

### §7.5 Income verification and reasonable compatibility
Verify current monthly income with pay stubs from the 30 days before filing, or state wage data (42 CFR 435.948). When the applicant's stated income and verified income are both at or below the limit, or within 10% of each other, they are **reasonably compatible** and no further documentation is required (42 CFR 435.952(c)). A stale pay stub (more than 30 days old) does not verify current income.

### §7.6 Timeliness
Decide non-disability Medicaid applications within 45 days of the filing date (42 CFR 435.912(c)(3)).
