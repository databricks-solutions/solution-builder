EU REACH Compliance Note

Regulatory Affairs · LuxeBeauty Skincare · Issue date 2026-Q1

Purpose

This note summarizes LuxeBeauty's compliance posture under EU REACH (Regulation EC 1907/2006) and the EU Cosmetics Regulation 1223/2009 for the Skincare product family sold in the EU/EEA. It is intended for internal regulatory training and audit reference.

Scope

Skincare actives, emulsifiers, preservatives, fragrances, and colorants used in EU-distributed LuxeBeauty SKUs. Excludes finished-product CPNP notifications (handled by the Responsible Person process).

Substance review

All Skincare actives currently in the SKU master are reviewed against the latest ECHA Candidate List for SVHC. As of 2026-Q1, no substance on the candidate list is present above the 0.1% w/w threshold in any LuxeBeauty Skincare formulation.

  • Phenoxyethanol: present below 1.0% (cosmetic limit), no further action required.
  • Iron oxides: ECHA-registered colorants, supplier COA on file.
  • Hyaluronic acid: not subject to REACH restriction at intended-use concentrations.
  • Plant extracts: covered under simplified registration scheme.

Responsible Person

For EU/EEA placing-on-the-market: LuxeBeauty SAS, Paris. Address and electronic contact registered with the ECHA notification portal under reference LB-RP-2024-001.

Review cadence

Substance posture is reviewed quarterly. The next ECHA candidate-list update is expected 2026-06.
