SOP-PACK-007 — Skincare Packaging Line

Standard Operating Procedure · Revision 4 · Effective 2025-09-01

Scope

This SOP covers the bottle-fill, cap-torque, label-application, and carton-seal stages of the Skincare packaging line. It applies to all primary-pack SKUs across the Lyon and Milan facilities. Packaging is independent of upstream emulsification; packaging-stage findings are tracked separately from formulation incidents.

QC sampling

  • Fill weight: 1 unit per 50 sampled, AQL 0.65.
  • Torque: 1 per 100 sampled, AQL 1.0.
  • Label position: 100% visual (camera).
  • Seal integrity: leak-test 1 per 100, AQL 0.65.

Rework

Reject units route to the rework cart; QC re-verifies before merge back into the batch. Rework rate target: <1.5% of batch volume; sustained excursions above 2% trigger an SOP review.

Line uptime

Packaging line uptime year-to-date: 96.4%. The dominant downtime category is changeover (SKU swaps); mechanical failures contribute <1% of unscheduled stops.
