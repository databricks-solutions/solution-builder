# Halcyra SOP CLINOPS-014 — Protocol Amendments and Enrollment Rescue (v6)

## 1. Scope
Applies to all Halcyra interventional studies. Covers substantial protocol amendments and the enrollment rescue plan
required when a study falls **more than 20% behind its enrollment plan** for two consecutive monthly reviews.

## 2. Enrollment rescue plan — required content
An enrollment rescue plan must contain: (a) root cause of the shortfall with evidence from the screening log;
(b) any proposed eligibility change with its scientific justification and a biostatistics impact memo;
(c) the list of **sites to add**, ranked, with expected contribution; (d) sites to **close or deprioritize**
(activated sites with zero randomizations after 6 months of activation are candidates for closure);
(e) a revised enrollment projection and LPI date; (f) approvals.

## 3. Approvals
- The **Study Manager** approves the rescue plan and the site add-list and records the decision in the study system.
- A **substantial amendment** additionally requires sign-off from the Medical Monitor, Biostatistics and the VP Clinical Operations.

## 4. Substantial amendments
4.1 A change to inclusion/exclusion criteria is a substantial amendment. It is submitted to FDA as an IND protocol
amendment (21 CFR 312.30(b)) and may be implemented once submitted and IRB-approved.
4.2 A biostatistics impact memo is required for any eligibility change (effect on power, event rate, stratification).
4.3 **Timelines:** central IRB review ≈ 30 days; local IRB review ≈ 60 days. Plan for the amendment to be effective at
central-IRB sites ~6 weeks and at local-IRB sites ~10 weeks after sign-off.
4.4 Participants already randomized are not re-screened. Participants who screen-failed only because of the amended
criterion **may be re-screened** once under the new protocol version.
4.5 Cost benchmark: a substantial Phase III amendment costs about $0.5M to implement and adds ~3 months to study
duration on average (industry benchmark) — far less than the cost of a year of enrollment delay.

## 5. Adding rescue sites
5.1 Prefer sites with: central IRB (faster start-up), prior Halcyra or strong nephrology enrollment history, an eligible
patient pool of ≥ 300 within 30 miles under the protocol version that will be in force, and **no competing trial in the
same population**.
5.2 Rescue sites must not compromise the study's **diversity action plan**: the combined enrolled population should stay
within 5 points of the plan's targets (Black or African American ≥ 18%, Hispanic or Latino ≥ 15%).
5.3 New sites are activated directly on the amended protocol version.
