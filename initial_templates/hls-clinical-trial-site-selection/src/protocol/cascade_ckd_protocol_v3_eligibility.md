# CASCADE-CKD Clinical Study Protocol HAL-301 — Version 3.0 (excerpt: Sections 4–6)

**Sponsor:** Halcyra Therapeutics, Inc. (fictional) · **Investigational product:** velmeraptan 10 mg / 20 mg oral, once daily (non-steroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist)
**Title:** A Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven study of velmeraptan on kidney outcomes in adults with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease
**Protocol version:** 3.0 (current). v1.0 approved 2023; v2.0 administrative; v3.0 added a central-lab UACR repeat window. No amendment has changed eligibility.

## 4. Study design
- Target: **1,200 randomized participants** at approximately **72 US sites**, 1:1 velmeraptan vs placebo on top of standard of care.
- Planned enrollment period: **24 months** from first patient in (FPI). Follow-up continues until **368 primary endpoint events** are accrued.
- Primary endpoint: time to first occurrence of sustained ≥ 40% decline in eGFR from baseline, end-stage kidney disease (ESKD), or renal death.
- Randomization is stratified by eGFR category (25–<45, 45–<60) and UACR category (300–<1000, ≥ 1000 mg/g).

## 5. Selection of study population

### 5.1 Inclusion criteria
- **I1** Adults aged 18 to 85 years with a documented diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus.
- **I2** eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) ≥ 25 and < 60 mL/min/1.73m² at screening. *Rationale: moderate-to-severe CKD provides the renal event rate the sample size assumes.*
- **I3** Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) ≥ 300 and < 5000 mg/g, first-morning void, central laboratory. One repeat is allowed within the screening window (added in v3.0). *Rationale: enriches for progressors.*
- **I4** HbA1c ≤ 10.5% at screening. *Rationale: avoids uncontrolled diabetes needing rescue therapy changes during run-in.*
- **I5** On a maximum tolerated, stable dose of an ACE inhibitor or ARB for ≥ 4 weeks before screening.

### 5.2 Exclusion criteria
- **E1** Known non-diabetic kidney disease (e.g. IgA nephropathy, polycystic kidney disease).
- **E2** Dialysis within 12 weeks of screening or prior kidney transplant.
- **E3** Myocardial infarction, stroke, or hospitalization for heart failure within 12 weeks.
- **E4** Treatment with a sodium-glucose co-transporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitor (e.g. empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, canagliflozin) **within 12 weeks prior to screening**.
- **E5** Treatment with a steroidal MRA (spironolactone, eplerenone) or non-steroidal MRA (finerenone) within 4 weeks.
- **E6** Child-Pugh B or C hepatic impairment.
- **E7** Serum potassium **> 4.8 mmol/L** at screening.

### 5.3 Rationale for exclusion E4 (SGLT2 inhibitors)
E4 was written in 2023 when SGLT2 inhibitors were not yet routine in diabetic kidney disease. Its purpose is to avoid
confounding the renal endpoint with a second renoprotective agent whose effect is of similar magnitude. The 2023
feasibility assessment assumed that about **15%** of otherwise-eligible patients would be taking an SGLT2 inhibitor.
There is no safety reason for E4: the combination of an MRA and an SGLT2 inhibitor has been studied in other programs
and SGLT2 inhibitors are associated with a *lower* incidence of hyperkalemia with MR antagonism. E4 is therefore an
**efficacy-design criterion, not a safety criterion**, and may be revisited by amendment (see SAP §9.4 and SOP CLINOPS-014).

### 5.4 Rationale for exclusion E7 (potassium)
Hyperkalemia is the principal identified risk of MR antagonism. The 4.8 mmol/L limit is conservative; the
Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) charter permits a change to ≤ 5.0 mmol/L only after the first unblinded safety
review, which is scheduled after 600 participants have 6 months of exposure. **E7 cannot be amended before that review.**

## 6. Concomitant medications
- 6.1 Required background: ACE inhibitor or ARB at maximum tolerated dose (I5).
- 6.2 Prohibited at screening: SGLT2 inhibitors (E4), other MRAs (E5), potassium supplements, potassium-sparing diuretics.
- 6.3 After randomization, an SGLT2 inhibitor may be started by the treating physician if clinically indicated;
  this is recorded as a concomitant medication and does not require discontinuation of study drug.
  (Note: this means SGLT2 inhibitor use is already expected in part of the randomized population over follow-up.)
