# CASCADE-CKD Feasibility Assessment (2023) — Summary

**Prepared by:** Halcyra Clinical Operations, Feasibility & Site Intelligence · **Status:** basis for the v1.0 enrollment plan

## Assumptions used for the enrollment plan
| Assumption | Value used | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Screen-failure rate | **35%** | Historical DKD Phase III benchmarks |
| Share of otherwise-eligible patients on an SGLT2 inhibitor (excluded by E4) | **~15%** | 2022 EHR network snapshot |
| Randomization rate per active site | 1.0 patient / site / month at steady state | Halcyra nephrology history |
| Number of sites | 72 US sites | |
| Site activation (selection → activation) | 120 days median | Prior Halcyra studies, central IRB |
| Competing Phase III DKD trials in the US | None expected before 2027 | Registry scan |

## Site selection approach (2023)
Sites were chosen for **diabetic kidney disease prevalence**: the Southeast and South Central regions were weighted most
heavily (about 60% of selected sites) because DKD prevalence and the proportion of Black and Hispanic patients — relevant to
the study's diversity plan — are highest there. The Midwest and Mountain West networks were used sparingly.

## Known risks identified in 2023
1. "If SGLT2 inhibitors become standard of care in DKD during enrollment, E4 could materially shrink the eligible pool.
   Re-assess at the first enrollment review." (Risk R-04 — owner: Study Manager.)
2. Local-IRB academic sites historically take 60–90 days longer to activate than central-IRB sites.
3. A competing program in the same population would reduce per-site randomization at shared sites.
