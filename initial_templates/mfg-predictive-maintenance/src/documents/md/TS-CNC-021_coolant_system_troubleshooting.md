# TS-CNC-021 — CNC Coolant System Pressure Loss: Troubleshooting

TechRobotics Industries · Line 1 Precision Machining · Haas UMC-750 / DMG MORI NLX 2500 / Okuma GENOS M560-V

## Symptoms
- Through-spindle coolant pressure falling below 4.2 bar (normal 4.4–4.8 bar) over days.
- Spindle and tool temperature creeping up +3 to +6 °C; surface-finish rejects on bores.

## Most likely causes (in order)
1. **Clogged coolant suction filter** (chip packing) — 70% of cases. Replace filter element HAAS-93-1000547 (in stock, 20 min).
2. Worn coolant pump impeller — replace pump HAAS-30-12890 (1.5 h).
3. Leaking rotary union.

## Action
If pressure is > 8% below baseline: replace the suction filter at the next Sunday window or at the next tool change (no line stop needed). If pressure keeps falling after the filter change, schedule pump replacement. A cavitating pump fails within 1–2 weeks.
