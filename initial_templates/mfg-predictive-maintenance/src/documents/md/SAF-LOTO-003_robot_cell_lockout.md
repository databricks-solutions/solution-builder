# SAF-LOTO-003 — Robot Cell Lockout/Tagout

TechRobotics Industries · EHS · Applies to all Line 2 robot cells

Before any reducer, servo or dress-pack work: stop the program, switch the controller to T1, open the cell gate, apply personal locks on the controller main disconnect and the pneumatic dump valve, verify zero energy (try-start), and support the arm for J2/J3 work (gravity load). Two-person rule for reducer replacement. Remove locks only after the cell is cleared and the robot is homed at 10% speed.
