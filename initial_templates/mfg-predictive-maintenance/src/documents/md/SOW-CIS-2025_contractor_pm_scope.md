# SOW-CIS-2025 — Contractor Scope of Work: Preventive Maintenance Services

Between TechRobotics Industries (Greenville Plant 2) and Carolina Industrial Services (CIS). Term: 2025–2027.

## 1. Scope
CIS performs overflow preventive maintenance on Line 2 robots and Line 1 CNC machines when in-house capacity is exceeded, under TechRobotics work orders.

## 2. Lubricants and consumables
- CIS shall use **only OEM-specified lubricants** as listed in the asset's engineering standard (for FANUC robots: ES-ROB-014, Vigo RE0).
- **Substitution of any lubricant requires written approval from TechRobotics Reliability Engineering before the work starts.** Unapproved substitutions are a non-conformance; CIS bears the cost of corrective work.
- CIS shall record the exact lubricant brand and batch in the work-order notes.

## 3. Quality
- Purge verification (≥ 95%) is mandatory on reducer grease jobs.
- Non-conformances are handled through the CAPA process; repeat non-conformance triggers a contract review.

## 4. Rates
Contractor PM labor is billed at a premium of about $380 per robot PM visit over in-house cost.
