# MM-PRS-009 — Servo Press Hydraulic Overload System Maintenance

TechRobotics Industries · Line 3 Press & Test · AIDA NS2-1500 and Schuler MSD 400

## Overload protection
The hydraulic overload protection holds 205–215 bar. A slow pressure decay (> 5% below baseline) indicates seal extrusion of the overload cylinder.

## Actions
- Replace the overload seal kit (AIDA-OLP-1500) and hydraulic filter element (PRS-HF-10) — 5 h, planned in the Wednesday night window.
- Crank bearing: lubricate per schedule; vibration > 4.5 mm/s with temperature rise indicates lubrication starvation (clogged line).
