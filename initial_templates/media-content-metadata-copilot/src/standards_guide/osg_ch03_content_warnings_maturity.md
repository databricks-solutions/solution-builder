# Orbitline+ Metadata & Content Standards Guide — Chapter 3: Content warnings and maturity

**§3.1 Evidence rule.** A content warning is applied only when a scene explicitly depicts it on screen, and the scene
(timecode) must be recorded as evidence. Warnings are never inferred from genre or tone.

**§3.2 Definitions.**
- Violence — a character is physically hurt on screen. Graphic Violence — blood, gore or a killing shown in close-up.
- Strong Language — explicit profanity. Sexual Content / Nudity — as depicted.
- Drug Use — illegal drugs consumed on screen (smuggling alone is not drug use). Alcohol Use / Smoking — as depicted.
- Self-Harm / Suicide — depiction or explicit discussion.
- Frightening Scenes — content designed to frighten a young child: menace, screaming, fire, monsters, jump scares.
- Death of a Parent — a parent's death shown or told on screen. Animal Harm. Flashing Lights (photosensitivity).

**§3.3 Warnings are mandatory before going live.** Titles whose warnings have not been reviewed must not be placed in
kids or family rows and must not be sold as Family-Safe ad inventory (see §4.3).

**§3.4 Maturity floor.** A title with Frightening Scenes or Death of a Parent must be rated at least TV-PG and may not
appear in "Little Ones (under 7)". A title with Violence, Strong Language, Alcohol Use or Smoking must be rated at
least TV-PG / PG. A title with any Restricted warning (Chapter 4) must be rated at least TV-14 / PG-13, and Sexual
Content, Nudity or Graphic Violence at least TV-MA / R unless Standards & Practices grants an exception.

**§3.5 Kids rows.** "Little Ones (under 7)" admits only Animation or Family titles rated TV-G or below with NO content
warnings. "Family Movie Night" admits Family or Animation titles rated PG or below; Frightening Scenes and Death of a
Parent are allowed there only if the warning is displayed on the title page.

**§3.6 Remediation.** When a missing warning is found on a live title, the editor must add the warning, correct the
maturity rating per §3.4, remove the title from any row it no longer qualifies for, and notify Ad Operations if the
brand-safety tier changes (§4.4). Target: same business day for kids-row titles.
