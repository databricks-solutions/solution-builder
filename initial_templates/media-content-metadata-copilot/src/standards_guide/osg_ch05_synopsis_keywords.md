# Orbitline+ Metadata & Content Standards Guide — Chapter 5: Synopses and keywords

**§5.1 Synopsis.** Two sentences, at most 60 words, present tense, spoiler-free (never reveal the ending, a death that
happens after the first act, or a twist). Name the lead character and the setting in the first sentence; state the
central conflict in the second. No marketing superlatives ("the best thriller of the year").

**§5.2 One-line vendor synopses** ("A man returns home.", "Feature film. 112 min.") do not meet the standard and must
be rewritten before a title is promoted.

**§5.3 Keywords.** 5 to 8 lowercase search keywords: setting, central themes, notable subject matter. No talent names
(talent has its own field), no genre names already tagged, no spoilers.

**§5.4 Localisation.** The English synopsis is the master; localised synopses are translated from it after approval.
