# Orbitline+ Metadata & Content Standards Guide — Chapter 6: Clip repurposing and the approval workflow

**§6.1 Promo clips.** Clips cut from a title for trailers, social and in-app collections must be 30 to 150 seconds,
must not reveal the ending or a major twist, and carry the title's content warnings. A clip containing any Standard or
Restricted content may not be used in kids or family promotion.

**§6.2 Rights.** Orbitline Originals may be clipped freely. Studio-licensed titles require the studio's promo-rights
window. The Harborlight Library agreement allows promotional clips of up to 3 minutes per title for on-platform and
social promotion of the Orbitline+ service, with no additional approval.

**§6.3 Collections from clips.** Themed collections (e.g. "Stormy Seas & Coastal Towns") may be built from clips found
by scene search; each clip keeps a link to its title and timecodes.

**§6.4 Approval workflow.** AI-proposed metadata is a draft. The editor reviews every proposed change (genres, moods,
themes, warnings, talent, keywords, synopsis), may edit it, and approves or rejects it. On approval the change is
published to the live catalogue, the completeness score and brand-safety tier are recomputed, and an audit record
(editor, timestamp, before/after, AI model and evidence) is kept for 7 years.

**§6.5 The July 2026 re-tag pilot.** Editors hand re-tagged 40 Harborlight titles on 27 July 2026, taking about 50
minutes per title. In the four weeks after, those titles earned several times more impressions and play starts than
before, while untouched Harborlight titles stayed flat. Hand re-tagging the remaining ~380 titles would take roughly
300 editor-hours — the reason the AI-assisted workflow exists.
