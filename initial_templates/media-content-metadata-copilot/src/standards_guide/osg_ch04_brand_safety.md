# Orbitline+ Metadata & Content Standards Guide — Chapter 4: Brand safety and ad inventory

**§4.1 Brand-safety tiers.** Every title on the ad-supported plan carries one tier, derived from its content warnings:
- **Restricted** — any of Graphic Violence, Sexual Content, Nudity, Drug Use, Self-Harm or Suicide. Only advertisers
  who opted into Restricted inventory may run against it.
- **Standard** — any of Violence, Strong Language, Alcohol Use, Smoking, Frightening Scenes, Death of a Parent, Animal
  Harm or Flashing Lights (and none of the Restricted warnings). Most general advertisers.
- **Family-Safe** — no content warnings after review. Premium inventory sold to family and kids brands.

**§4.2 The tier is derived, never hand-set.** The tier follows mechanically from the reviewed warnings. Changing a
title's warnings changes its tier.

**§4.3 Unreviewed titles.** Legacy feeds default unreviewed titles to Family-Safe. This is a known risk: an unreviewed
title must be treated as unverified and held out of Family-Safe campaigns until its warnings are reviewed.

**§4.4 Tier changes.** When a tier moves from Family-Safe to Standard or Restricted, Ad Operations must be told so that
Family-Safe campaigns stop serving against the title. Makegoods may be owed to advertisers whose Family-Safe campaigns
ran against a title later found to be Standard or Restricted.
