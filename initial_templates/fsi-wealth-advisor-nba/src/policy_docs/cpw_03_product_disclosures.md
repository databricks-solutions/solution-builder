# Calder Pine Private Wealth — Required Product Disclosures
Revision 2026-3. Each code below must be delivered (and logged) before the client decides. The Advisor Morning Book lists the codes for every recommended action.

**D-CRS — Form CRS & fee disclosure.** Deliver the Form CRS (Client Relationship Summary) at or before the first recommendation of a new account type, strategy or rollover, and on request. States that Calder Pine may earn more on some products than others.

**D-CD — Bank CDs.** Calder Pine Bank CDs are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category. Early withdrawal penalties apply. The 12-month renewal rate on September 28, 2026 is **3.55%** (a CD bought in 2025 at 5.10% will NOT renew at the old rate).

**D-TSY — U.S. Treasury ladder.** Treasury bills and notes are backed by the full faith and credit of the U.S. government; prices fluctuate if sold before maturity. Interest is exempt from state income tax. A ladder spreads maturities (e.g. 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 months) so part of the money comes due regularly for spending needs.

**D-MMF — Government money market fund.** A money market fund is **not a bank deposit, not FDIC-insured** and may lose value, although it seeks to maintain a stable $1.00 share price. Current 7-day yield ~3.60% vs the 0.25% bank sweep rate. The firm's affiliate earns a management fee.

**D-ADV — Advisory program brochure (Form ADV Part 2A).** Managed portfolios are offered through the Calder Pine advisory program; describe the strategy, rebalancing and the manager.

**D-FEE — Advisory fee schedule.** Annual advisory fee 0.95% on the first $1M, 0.80% on the next $4M, 0.60% above $5M, billed quarterly.

**D-SN — Structured notes.** Buffered notes are unsecured obligations of the issuing bank (issuer credit risk), are not FDIC-insured, have limited or no liquidity before maturity, cap the upside, and embed fees. Complex product — see Complex Products Policy §3.

**D-PC — Private credit interval fund.** Illiquid: redemptions limited to 5% of fund shares per quarter. Credit and valuation risk. Complex product.

**D-DI — Direct indexing.** Tax-loss harvesting benefits depend on the client's tax situation; tracking error vs the index; wash-sale rules apply.

**D-ROLL — 401(k) rollover (DOL PTE 2020-02 / Reg BI).** Before recommending a rollover the advisor must deliver a written comparison of the plan vs the IRA: fees and expenses, services, investment options, penalty-free withdrawal rules, creditor protection, required minimum distributions, and the reason the rollover is in the client's best interest. Leaving the money in the plan, rolling to a new employer plan, or cashing out (not recommended) are the alternatives that must be described.

**D-REFI — Mortgage refinance (Calder Pine Bank, NMLS #402917).** Closing costs typically 1.5%-2.5% of the loan; break-even months = closing costs ÷ monthly savings. Rate quotes are estimates, not a commitment to lend; subject to credit approval and appraisal. Equal Housing Lender. Advisors may introduce the client to a Calder Pine Bank mortgage officer but may not quote a binding rate.

**D-SBLOC — Securities-based line of credit.** The client borrows against pledged securities. If the collateral value falls, Calder Pine may issue a **maintenance call** and may sell pledged securities without prior notice; the client may lose more than the amount borrowed. Proceeds may not be used to buy securities. Variable rate (SOFR + spread). A concentrated position receives a reduced advance rate (typically 50% instead of 70%). Complex product.

**D-529 — 529 plans.** Program description / offering statement; state tax benefits vary by state of residence; non-qualified withdrawals are taxed and incur a 10% penalty on earnings.
