# Calder Pine Private Wealth — Client Communications & Outreach Standards
Revision 2026-1. Implements FINRA Rule 2210 (communications with the public) for advisor emails, letters and call scripts.

§1 **Fair and balanced.** Every outreach must be fair and balanced and provide a sound basis for evaluating the facts. Never promise or project performance ("guaranteed", "can't lose", "risk-free"). A CD or Treasury yield may be stated as a current rate with its date.
§2 **Name the reason, not the product.** Lead with the client's situation (a maturing CD, cash in the account, a retirement, a family change) and invite a conversation. Product names and rates belong in the meeting, with the disclosures.
§3 **Vulnerable clients.** For a client within 12 months of a bereavement, the first outreach is a personal call; any follow-up email must be empathetic, short, and contain no product names or urgency beyond factual dates (Vulnerable Client Policy §2.4).
§4 **Required footer on every email:** "Calder Pine Private Wealth is the marketing name for Calder Pine Securities LLC (member FINRA/SIPC) and Calder Pine Advisors LLC, an SEC-registered investment adviser. Investment products are not FDIC-insured, not bank-guaranteed and may lose value. Bank deposits and loans are offered by Calder Pine Bank, Member FDIC, Equal Housing Lender."
§5 **Retention.** Every email and call script is archived (SEC Rule 17a-4). Drafts produced by AI tools must be reviewed and sent by the advisor; the advisor is responsible for the content.
§6 **Tone.** Plain English, under 150 words for an email, signed by the advisor with direct phone number.
