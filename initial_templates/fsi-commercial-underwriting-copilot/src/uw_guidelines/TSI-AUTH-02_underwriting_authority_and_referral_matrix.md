# TSI-AUTH-02 — Underwriting Authority & Referral Matrix

**Tidewell Specialty Insurance** (fictional) — Commercial Property & Package

## 1. Principle

Every underwriter acts within written authority. When a submission exceeds authority or needs an appetite exception,
the underwriter prepares the full recommendation (terms, premium, rationale) and refers it; the referral authority
approves, amends or sends it back. Referral does not transfer the work — it transfers the decision.

## 2. Authority by role (total insured value per account)

| Role | TIV authority | Appetite exceptions |
|---|---|---|
| Underwriter | up to $10M | none |
| Senior underwriter | up to **$25M** | none |
| Chief Underwriting Officer (CUO) | up to $75M | may grant exceptions to TSI-UW-01 §4 and to appetite bulletins |

Rule AUTH-TIV-SENIOR: an account whose TIV **after any exclusions** is above $25M must be approved by the CUO.

## 3. Mandatory referrals to the CUO regardless of TIV

3.1 Any exception to an appetite bulletin (e.g., TSI-UW-B-2026-07 roof age).
3.2 Protection class 9-10 locations (TSI-UW-01 §4.1); older frame habitational without sprinklers (TSI-UW-01 §4.3).
3.3 Quoting more than 10% below technical premium (TSI-RATE-03 §7).
3.4 Any account with an open property claim above $250,000.

## 4. What the referral must contain

- The recommended decision and terms (locations quoted and excluded, deductibles, conditions).
- The premium by location and the technical premium.
- Every discrepancy between the application and the documents, and how it was resolved.
- The guideline references relied on.
- The risk model score as submitted and as verified.

## 5. Service levels

The CUO responds to referrals within one business day. Declinations do not need a referral unless the broker disputes
them.
