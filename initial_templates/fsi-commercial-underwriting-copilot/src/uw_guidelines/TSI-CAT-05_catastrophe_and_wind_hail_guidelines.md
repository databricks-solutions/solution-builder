# TSI-CAT-05 — Catastrophe, Wind & Hail Guidelines

**Tidewell Specialty Insurance** (fictional) — Commercial Property & Package

## 1. Catastrophe appetite

Tidewell manages convective storm (hail, tornado, straight-line wind) and hurricane accumulations within the
reinsurance program. Named-storm exposure is written only inland.

## 2. Wind / hail deductibles

2.1 In the hail belt (TX, OK, KS, NE, CO) offer a 2% wind/hail deductible on every location; it is mandatory for
locations with roofs older than 15 years in any segment.
2.2 A 5% wind/hail deductible with Actual Cash Value roof settlement is mandatory for any habitational location written
under a CUO exception to TSI-UW-B-2026-07.
2.3 Percentage deductibles apply per location to the location's TIV, with a $25,000 minimum.

## 3. Coastal wind

3.1 **Florida Tier-1 coastal wind counties** (the first tier of counties on the Gulf and Atlantic coasts, e.g. Bay
County — Panama City Beach — and Miami-Dade) are **outside appetite for all segments**. Decline (rule AP-FL-COASTAL).
Refer the broker to the surplus-lines market.
3.2 Inland Florida locations are eligible at the Florida territory factor (TSI-RATE-03 §2.3).

## 4. Accumulation

Underwriters check the aggregate TIV within a 1-mile radius of existing policies for any single location above $20M.
