# TSI-UW-B-2026-07 — Appetite Bulletin: Hail-Belt Habitational Roof Age

**Tidewell Specialty Insurance — Commercial Property & Package**
Issued: 15 June 2026 by Marcus Oyelaran, Chief Underwriting Officer
Effective: new business and renewals with effective dates on or after **1 July 2026**
Supersedes: nothing (adds to TSI-UW-01 §4 Habitational)

## 1. Why this bulletin exists

Portfolio review of accident quarters 2025-Q2 through 2026-Q1 showed that the habitational segment in the hail belt
(Texas, Oklahoma, Kansas, Nebraska and Colorado) has become unprofitable, and that the deterioration is concentrated
in buildings with older roofs:

| Hail-belt habitational, trailing four quarters to 2026-Q1 | Loss ratio |
|---|---|
| Roofs older than 15 years | **109%** |
| Roofs 15 years or newer | 64% |
| Rest of the Tidewell commercial property book | 56% |

Hail and wind account for nearly nine out of ten dollars of loss on the older-roof buildings (87%). The trend is still
worsening quarter over quarter. Our rating plan (TSI-RATE-03) has only carried the full roof-age factor since the
2026 filing, so business written earlier is under-priced for this exposure.

## 2. The rule (rule ID AP-HAB-HAIL-ROOF)

2.1 Any **habitational location** (apartments, condominium associations, student housing) located in **TX, OK, KS, NE
or CO** with a **roof older than 15 years** (roof age = 2026 minus the year the roof covering was last fully replaced,
as shown on the statement of values) is **outside appetite for property coverage**.

2.2 The underwriter must, for each such location, do ONE of the following:

- (a) **Exclude the location** from the property schedule and quote the remaining locations; or
- (b) Obtain evidence that the roof has been fully replaced before the effective date (invoice or permit); or
- (c) **Refer to the Chief Underwriting Officer** for an exception. An exception, if granted, requires **Actual Cash Value
  (ACV) roof loss settlement** and a **5% wind/hail deductible** on that location (see TSI-RATE-03 §4.1 and TSI-CAT-05 §2).

2.3 The roof year on the **statement of values** governs. If the application and the SOV disagree, underwrite on the
SOV (see TSI-DOC-04 §3.2). A broker statement such as "roofs updated 2019-2020" does not satisfy 2.2(b).

2.4 Prior hail or wind losses at the same location within five years are a strong indicator that the roof is
deteriorated; note them in the file and in the referral.

## 3. What does not change

- Hail-belt habitational locations with roofs 15 years or newer remain in appetite at standard rates.
- Other segments in the hail belt are not affected by this bulletin; the roof-age rating factor still applies.
- The senior underwriter TIV authority limit (TSI-AUTH-02 §2, $25M) is unchanged; an account can require both an
  appetite referral and an authority referral.

## 4. Monitoring

The portfolio team reports hail-belt habitational loss ratio by roof band every quarter. The bulletin will be reviewed
after the 2026-Q3 results.
