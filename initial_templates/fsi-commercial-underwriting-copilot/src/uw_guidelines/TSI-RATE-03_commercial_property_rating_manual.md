# TSI-RATE-03 — Commercial Property Rating Manual (2026 filing)

**Tidewell Specialty Insurance** (fictional). Property premium is rated per location and summed; the package general
liability premium is added on revenue. All factors are multiplicative.

## 1. Formula

Location property premium = TIV / 100 x base rate (§2.1) x occupancy factor (§2.2) x territory factor (§2.3)
x roof-age factor (§2.4) x sprinkler factor (§2.5) x experience modifier (§3.1) x risk-model modifier (§3.2)
x deductible credit (§4.1, where elected).

Account premium = sum of location premiums (subject to the §5 minimum) + general liability premium (§6).

## 2. Base rates and exposure factors

2.1 **Base rate per $100 of TIV by construction:** Frame 0.34; Joisted masonry 0.25; Masonry non-combustible 0.20;
Fire resistive 0.15.

2.2 **Occupancy factor:** Habitational 1.10; Retail & Restaurants 1.00; Light Manufacturing 1.15; Warehouse &
Distribution 0.95; Office & Professional 0.85; Hospitality 1.20.

2.3 **Territory factor:** Hail belt (TX, OK, KS, NE, CO) 1.25; Florida (non-coastal counties) 1.45; all other states 1.00.

2.4 **Roof-age factor:** 10 years or newer 0.95; 11-15 years 1.05; 16-20 years 1.30; older than 20 years 1.50.
Full weight of this factor applies from the 2026 filing onward.

2.5 **Sprinkler factor:** fully sprinklered building 0.85; otherwise 1.00.

## 3. Account modifiers

3.1 **Experience modifier** (count of claims on five years of loss runs, all lines): no losses 0.92; one loss 1.00;
two or three losses 1.12; four or more 1.25.

3.2 **Risk-model modifier** (submission_risk_model@champion, scored on the verified SOV and loss runs — never on the
application alone): Low (p < 0.25) 0.95; Moderate (0.25 to 0.45) 1.00; High (p >= 0.45) 1.15.

## 4. Deductibles

4.1 **Wind/hail percentage deductibles (hail belt):** 2% of location TIV earns a 0.92 credit; 5% earns 0.82. A 5%
wind/hail deductible and Actual Cash Value roof settlement are mandatory on any location written under a CUO exception
to TSI-UW-B-2026-07.
4.2 The all-other-perils deductible is $25,000 per occurrence for accounts above $10M TIV, $10,000 below.

## 5. Minimum premium

Minimum property premium per account: **$7,500**.

## 6. General liability (package)

General liability premium = annual revenue / 1,000 x 0.62, minimum $2,500.

## 7. Price adequacy

The technical premium produced by this manual targets a 58% loss ratio. Quoting more than 10% below technical
requires CUO approval. A broker target premium is information, not a constraint.
