# TSI-DOC-04 — Submission Documentation Standards

**Tidewell Specialty Insurance** (fictional) — Commercial Property & Package

## 1. Required documents

1.1 **Application** in ACORD 125 (commercial insurance application) and ACORD 140 (property section) format, signed.
1.2 **Statement of values (SOV)** listing every location: address, construction, year built, year the roof was last
replaced, stories, units (habitational), sprinklers, protection class and replacement-cost TIV.
1.3 **Loss runs** from every carrier for the past five years.

## 2. Loss-run standards (rule AP-DOCS-LOSSRUN)

2.1 Loss runs must be **currently valued — valuation date within 90 days** of the date Tidewell received the
submission — and must cover **five full years**. Otherwise, request updated loss runs before quoting; do not issue an
indication on stale loss runs.
2.2 Loss runs must show every line (property and general liability), paid, reserve and total incurred, and claim status.

## 3. Reconciling the documents

3.1 The application is the broker's summary. The SOV and the loss runs are the evidence.
3.2 **Underwrite the documents, not the application** (rule AP-DOCS-MATCH). When the SOV or the loss runs contradict
the application — TIV, number of locations, construction, roof year, sprinklers, loss count or loss amounts — use the
SOV and loss-run values for eligibility, rating and the risk model, and record every discrepancy in the file.
3.3 Material discrepancies (a location outside appetite that the application did not disclose, or undisclosed losses
above $50,000) must be mentioned to the broker in the quote or declination letter.
3.4 AI extraction of submission documents (ai_parse_document + structured extraction) is accepted as the system of
record for intake, provided the underwriter reviews the extracted SOV and loss runs before issuing a decision.
