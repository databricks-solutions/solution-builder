# TSI-UW-01 — Commercial Property & Package Underwriting Guidelines

**Tidewell Specialty Insurance** (fictional) — Middle-market commercial property and package (property + general liability)
Version 2026.2 — owner: Chief Underwriting Officer

## 1. Target market

Tidewell writes middle-market commercial property and package business through appointed brokers: accounts with
$1M to $60M total insured value (TIV), one to ten locations, in the 13 states of our footprint (TX, OK, KS, NE, CO,
GA, NC, TN, AZ, IL, OH, PA, FL). Target account loss ratio: 55-60%.

## 2. Segments and appetite

| Segment | Appetite | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Office & Professional | Preferred | Owner-occupied or multi-tenant office, medical office. |
| Warehouse & Distribution | Preferred | Dry storage, cold storage (ammonia systems require annual inspection), cross-dock. |
| Retail & Restaurants | Standard | Restaurants need UL-300 hood suppression and semi-annual hood cleaning. |
| Light Manufacturing | Standard | No foundries, no woodworking, no chemical processing. |
| Habitational | Standard — restricted in the hail belt | See §4 and Appetite Bulletin TSI-UW-B-2026-07. |
| Hospitality | Selective | Limited-service and extended-stay hotels; no bars/nightclubs; see TSI-CAT-05 for coastal wind. |

## 3. General eligibility

3.1 Every submission must include a completed application (ACORD 125 and 140 format), a statement of values listing
every location, and currently valued loss runs (TSI-DOC-04).
3.2 Vacant or unoccupied buildings (below 30% occupancy) are ineligible.
3.3 Buildings under renovation above 25% of replacement cost require a builder's risk referral.

## 4. Property risk quality

4.1 **Public protection.** Locations in ISO public protection class 9 or 10 require a referral to the Chief
Underwriting Officer (rule AP-PROTECTION).
4.2 **Roofs.** Roof age is the single largest driver of wind and hail loss. Roof age is measured from the year the roof
covering was last fully replaced, per the statement of values. Roofs older than 20 years anywhere require a roof
condition report. In the hail belt, habitational roofs older than 15 years are governed by TSI-UW-B-2026-07.
4.3 **Older frame habitational.** Frame-construction habitational buildings built before 1990 without automatic
sprinklers require a CUO referral (rule AP-HAB-FRAME-OLD); the file must document fire separations and alarm
monitoring.
4.4 **Sprinklers.** Automatic sprinklers earn the sprinkler credit only when the whole building is protected.

## 5. Loss history

5.1 Underwrite on five years of loss history from the prior carrier's loss runs.
5.2 Two or more property losses at the same location in five years, or any single property loss above 10% of that
location's TIV, requires the underwriter to explain in the file why the cause has been corrected.
5.3 An account whose five-year incurred losses exceed 1.5x the expiring five-year property premium is outside the
fast-track lane.

## 6. Decisions and documentation

6.1 Every quote, conditional quote or declination is recorded in the underwriting workbench with the rationale and the
guideline references relied on.
6.2 A decline must cite the specific guideline. Offer the broker an alternative (e.g., excluding a location) when the
rest of the account is in appetite.
6.3 Quotes are valid for 30 days and subject to the conditions stated on the quote.
