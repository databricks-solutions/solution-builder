# Supplier Quality Agreement SQA-HAN-2024-07 — Brightwater Cycle Works and Hanlin Electronics Co.

Signed 2024-07-15. Applies to all production parts Hanlin supplies to Brightwater (MC-48 controller family).

## §4 Quality requirements
4.1 Zero-defect target; field-failure target ≤ 2 controller warranty claims per 1,000 units per 12 months in service.
4.2 Supplier maintains IATF 16949-aligned process controls and the control plan approved at PPAP.

## §5 Change management
5.1 The supplier shall not change materials, sub-suppliers, process, location or tooling without prior written
approval from Brightwater Supplier Quality.
5.2 **Class 1 changes** (any change affecting a Critical characteristic in the approved PPAP, including conformal
coating material or thickness) **require a new PPAP submission and written approval before the first shipment.**
5.3 Parts shipped under an unapproved change are **nonconforming**, regardless of the supplier's own impact
assessment.

## §6 Containment and problem solving
6.1 On notice of a suspected field issue the supplier shall implement containment within **24 hours** (stop
shipment, sort / quarantine stock at supplier, in transit and at Brightwater).
6.2 The supplier shall deliver an **8D report**: D3 containment within 24 h, D4 root cause within 10 business days,
D5–D7 permanent corrective action and verification within 30 days.

## §8 Warranty cost recovery (chargeback)
8.1 For field failures attributable to a supplier part, the supplier reimburses Brightwater for **100 % of the part
cost and dealer labor** at Brightwater's warranty labor rate (**$96 per hour**, times per the Brightwater Warranty
Labor Time Guide), plus an **administrative fee of $35 per claim**.
8.2 **Unapproved changes:** where failures result from a change made without the approval required by §5, the supplier
additionally reimburses **100 % of field-campaign costs** (campaign parts, dealer labor, logistics, customer
notification) and **100 % of the cost of sorting and replacing quarantined stock**.
8.3 Chargebacks are submitted quarterly (or per campaign) with claim-level evidence: serial number, lot genealogy,
technician findings and failure analysis of returned parts. The supplier has 30 days to dispute with evidence;
undisputed amounts are paid within 60 days or offset against open invoices.
8.4 Recovery is limited to failures within Brightwater's 24-month consumer warranty.

## §9 Escalation
Repeat or unresolved issues move the supplier to **Controlled Shipping Level 1** (supplier-paid 100 % inspection) and
may trigger new-business hold.
