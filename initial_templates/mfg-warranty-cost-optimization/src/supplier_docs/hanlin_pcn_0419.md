# Process Change Notification PCN-0419 — Hanlin Electronics Co. (MC-48 controller)

**PCN number:** PCN-0419 · **Date issued:** 2026-04-02 · **Supplier:** Hanlin Electronics Co. (SUP-HAN)
**Affected part:** MC-48 hub-motor controller, Brightwater P/N 30-4801 · **Proposed effective lot:** HX-2605
(production from 2026-04-28)

## 1. Description of change (as submitted by the supplier)
Due to an allocation shortage of the AC-220 acrylic coating, Hanlin will change the conformal coating to
**SC-15 silicone-modified coating applied at 25–35 µm** (previously AC-220 acrylic at 50–75 µm), using a
dip process instead of selective spray. The cure profile changes to 20 min at 80 °C.

## 2. Supplier's impact assessment
Hanlin classified the change as **"minor — no impact on form, fit or function"** and proposed to ship without a new
PPAP. Supporting evidence provided: a 48 h humidity test on 3 boards (pass). No damp-heat 1,000 h, no salt-spray
and no thermal-shock re-test was performed.

## 3. Brightwater response (SQE Lena Ortiz, 2026-04-09)
The change affects critical characteristics CC-1 and CC-2 of the approved PPAP (BWC-PPAP-HAN-MC48-2024). It is a
**Class 1 change under SQA-HAN-2024-07 §5.2**. Brightwater **does not approve** shipment under PCN-0419 until a full
**PPAP Level 3 re-submission** is received and approved, including 1,000 h damp heat (85/85), ASTM B117 96 h salt spray
and IP65 water-jet + thermal shock on production-intent boards. Until approval, Hanlin must continue shipping the
approved AC-220 configuration or request a written deviation.

## 4. Status log
- 2026-04-09 — Brightwater rejects shipment without PPAP; re-submission requested by 2026-05-15.
- 2026-05-15 — Re-submission **not received**. Hanlin states "test lab capacity delayed".
- 2026-06-20 — Re-submission still **not received**. No written deviation was requested or granted.
- Lots HX-2605, HX-2606, HX-2607, HX-2608 and HX-2609 were shipped to the Reno plant with the SC-15 coating
  (confirmed from Hanlin certificates of conformance, which list "SC-15 / PCN-0419").

**Conclusion:** lots HX-2605 onward were produced and shipped under an **unapproved Class 1 process change**.
