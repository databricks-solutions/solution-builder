# Brightwater Cycle Works — Dealer Warranty Policy and Labor Time Guide (rev. 2026-01)

## 1. Coverage
Every Brightwater e-bike carries a **24-month limited warranty** from the retail sale date covering defects in
materials and workmanship of the frame, motor, controller, battery, charger, display and brakes. Wear items (brake
pads, chains, cassettes, tires) are covered only for manufacturing defects.

## 2. Filing a claim
Dealers file within 30 days of repair with: serial number, odometer, customer complaint, **technician notes describing
what was physically found**, dealer labor code and the replaced part (returned on request for failure analysis).
Dealer labor codes: ELEC-GEN (general electrical), MOTOR, BATT, BRAKE, DISPLAY, DRIVE, CHARGER, FRAME, OTHER.
Labor is paid at **$96/hour**.

## 3. Labor Time Guide (selected operations)
| Operation | Code | Standard time |
|---|---|---|
| Motor controller remove & replace (MC-48 hub, MC-60 mid-drive) | ELEC-GEN / MOTOR | **1.8 h** |
| Controller seal kit + re-coat inspection (Service Campaign SC-26-07 procedure) | ELEC-GEN | **0.3 h** |
| Hub motor R&R | MOTOR | 1.5 h |
| Mid-drive unit R&R | MOTOR | 2.2 h |
| Battery pack replace | BATT | 1.0 h |
| Brake caliper replace + bleed | BRAKE | 1.2 h |
| Firmware reflash (display / controller) | DISPLAY | 0.6 h |

## 4. Parts pricing to dealers (warranty)
MC-48 controller $312 · MC-60 controller $348 · controller seal kit (gasket G-48B + dielectric grease + potting
compound) **$22** · battery pack BP-500 $640 · brake caliper $96.

## 5. Goodwill
Repeat failures (same symptom twice within 90 days) qualify for a goodwill accessory credit of $75.
