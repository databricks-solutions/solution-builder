# QP-12 Field Action and Service Campaign Procedure — Brightwater Cycle Works

## 1. Triggers
A Field Quality Review is required when an early-warning signal shows a failure mode at **≥ 3× its baseline claims
per 1,000 units** with at least 15 claims, OR projected remaining-warranty exposure above **$250,000**, OR any
potential safety hazard.

## 2. Safety assessment
Sudden loss of motor assist is a **degraded-performance** condition (the bike remains a pedal bicycle with working
brakes), not a fire, fall or brake hazard. It does not by itself require a CPSC report, but the product-safety lead
must document the assessment. Any report of thermal events, smoke or brake failure escalates to a safety recall
review within 24 hours.

## 3. Containment options (choose all that apply)
1. **Stop-ship / quarantine**: hold affected components at the plant and affected bikes in dealer stock (sold=false).
2. **Supplier containment**: 24 h containment and 8D per the SQA; sort suspect lots at the supplier's cost.
3. **Service campaign** (dealer-performed, customer-notified): apply the controller **seal kit (gasket G-48B, dielectric
   grease, edge potting)** — 0.3 h labor + $22 kit ($50.80 per bike at the $96/h labor rate) — to every affected bike in the field and in stock. Engineering
   validation (lab 2026-09): the seal kit reduces moisture ingress failures on SC-15-coated controllers by **~85 %**.
4. **Controller replacement campaign**: replace the controller with an approved-configuration part (1.8 h + $312) —
   reserve for bikes already showing fault E21.
5. **Firmware mitigation**: firmware cannot prevent corrosion; it may only add a moisture warning.

## 4. Prioritization
Campaign waves are sequenced by risk: wave 1 = bikes in wet-climate regions (Pacific Northwest, Northeast,
Southeast) and any connected bike that has reported fault E21; wave 2 = all remaining affected units and dealer stock.

## 5. Cost recovery
Campaign costs are charged back to the supplier when the root cause is a supplier nonconformance
(see the supplier's SQA §8). Quality opens a **Quality Case** that records root cause, affected population, containment
actions, projected exposure and the chargeback amount.
