# High-Risk Jurisdictions & Cross-Border Wire Policy (Policy HRJ-02)

Fictional internal policy for Northwind Federal Bank.

## Elevated-risk jurisdiction list (reviewed quarterly)
The bank applies enhanced monitoring to wires involving: **United Arab Emirates (AE)**, **Hong Kong (HK)**, Panama (PA), Cyprus (CY), Turkey (TR), Nigeria (NG), Venezuela (VE), Cayman Islands (KY), British Virgin Islands (VG). The list combines FATF grey-list countries, FinCEN advisories and the bank's own SAR history (free-trade-zone trading companies are over-represented in prior filings).

## Enhanced due diligence triggers
- Any single wire >= **$50,000** to an elevated-risk jurisdiction from a customer relationship younger than 12 months.
- Cumulative wires >= **$250,000** in 60 days to elevated-risk jurisdictions.
- Beneficiary is a **free-zone entity** (e.g. "FZE", "FZCO") or a holding company with no public trade footprint.
- Stated purpose (e.g. "vehicle export invoice") not supported by shipping documents, bills of lading or customs filings.

## Trade-based money laundering (TBML) indicators
- Used-vehicle exports paid with cash collected domestically from third parties.
- Round or near-round invoice amounts; invoices that repeat with minor variation.
- Payments to a third-party beneficiary in a jurisdiction different from the buyer.
- Business account funded mostly by internal transfers from other customers rather than by customer payments.

## Required action
Investigator documents the business rationale; if not established, escalate to BSA Officer and consider SAR. Wires above $100K to elevated-risk jurisdictions linked to a SAR case require BSA Officer escalation.
