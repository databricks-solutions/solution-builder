# Aeralis Airways — Unaccompanied Minors and Passengers Needing Special Assistance during IROPS

Document SSR-04 · Version 5 · Customer Service Standards

## 1. SSR codes the IROPS desk sees
UMNR unaccompanied minor · WCHR wheelchair for distance (can walk short distances and climb stairs) · WCHS cannot climb
stairs · WCHC immobile, needs an aisle chair to the seat · BLND blind or low vision · DEAF deaf or hard of hearing · MEDA
medical case · INFT travelling with a lap infant (under 2) · PETC pet in cabin.

## 2. Unaccompanied minors (UMNR)
2.1 Mandatory UMNR service for children aged 5 to 14 travelling without a passenger aged 16 or older.
2.2 **During a cancellation a UMNR is never left overnight** — not in the terminal and not in a hotel. The desk must either:
    (a) re-accommodate the child **the same day on an Aeralis flight** with UMNR service, or
    (b) re-accommodate on **Lumen Air only if the child is 8 or older and the Lumen Air flight is a nonstop** (interline UMNR
        agreement; Lumen Air does not accept UMNR under 8 or on connections), or
    (c) return the child to the guardian who dropped them off at the airport.
2.3 The desk calls **both guardians** (dropping-off and receiving) before a change is final and confirms the receiving
    guardian can meet the new arrival. A staff escort stays with the child until handover.
2.4 A UMNR can never be moved to a nearby airport alternate.

## 3. Passengers needing special assistance (PRM)
3.1 Re-accommodate first (policy OPS-IROPS-01 §2.1), keeping their travelling companion(s) on the same flight.
3.2 WCHC passengers need an aircraft with an on-board aisle chair and cannot be sent to a nearby-airport alternate.
3.3 If an overnight is unavoidable: an accessible hotel room from the block, accessible transport, and wheelchair assistance
    at the hotel curb and on return. A PRM is never left overnight in the terminal.
3.4 Mobility devices: confirm the new aircraft's cargo door can take a power wheelchair (A320-family and larger: yes;
    E175: batteries must be removable).

## 4. Families with a lap infant (INFT)
Re-accommodate with the highest priority; a family with a lap infant is entitled to a hotel room if an overnight is
unavoidable (CARE-02 §3.2). Keep the family together.
