# Aeralis Airways — Fare Rules and the Winter Storm Harlan Travel Waiver

Document FARE-11 · Revenue Management & Pricing

## 1. Fare brands (normal rules)
| Brand | Changes | Cancellation | Seat selection | Partner rebooking during IROPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Not permitted | Not refundable (credit not offered) | Paid | Yes — IROPS overrides the brand |
| Main | Free change, pay fare difference | Travel credit | Standard free | Yes |
| Comfort+ | Free change, pay fare difference | Travel credit | Extra legroom | Yes |
| First / Business | Free change | Refundable | Included | Yes, same cabin where available |

During IROPS every brand, including Basic, may be re-accommodated on own-metal or Lumen Air flights at no cost to the
passenger. A passenger rebooked into a lower cabin receives the fare difference as a refund (REG-US-04 §2).

## 2. Travel waiver WX-HARLAN-0213 (issued 12 Feb 2026, 18:00 CT)
- Applies to tickets issued on or before 12 Feb 2026 for travel to, from or through **Chicago O'Hare (ORD)** on
  **13, 14 or 15 February 2026**.
- Passengers may change to travel on or before **20 February 2026** in the same cabin with **no change fee and no fare
  difference**, whether or not their flight was cancelled.
- If the flight is **cancelled** or significantly delayed and the passenger chooses not to travel, a **full refund** to the
  original form of payment is available on request (and automatically if they decline the rebooking — REG-US-04).
- Travel after 20 February: the change fee is waived but a fare difference may apply.

## 3. Partner (Lumen Air) re-accommodation
Aeralis tickets can be re-issued on Lumen Air under the interline IROPS agreement at the agreed per-seat rate
(protected partner seats for Harlan: USD 180–340 domestic, USD 610–675 transatlantic via Newark). The passenger pays
nothing. Loyalty miles are credited as if flown on Aeralis.
