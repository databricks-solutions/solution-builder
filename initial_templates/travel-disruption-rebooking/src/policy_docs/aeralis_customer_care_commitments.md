# Aeralis Airways — Customer Care Commitments during Delays and Cancellations

Document CARE-02 · Effective 1 October 2025 · Published on aeralis.example/commitments

Aeralis Airways is a fictional carrier. These commitments are Aeralis policy; where a law gives the passenger more
(EU261, UK261, US DOT refund rules), the law applies.

## 1. Controllable vs uncontrollable
- **Controllable** causes: crew scheduling, maintenance, IT outages, aircraft cleaning, fueling, baggage loading.
- **Uncontrollable** causes: weather (including winter storms, de-icing restrictions and ground stops caused by weather),
  air traffic control programs, airport closures, security events.
- A cancellation caused by an aircraft or crew being out of position because of weather is treated as **uncontrollable**.

## 2. Meals
A meal voucher of **USD 15 per passenger for every 3 hours of delay** to the new arrival, for any cause (guardrail G7),
up to 3 vouchers per day of delay.

## 3. Hotel accommodation
3.1 Controllable cancellation with an overnight stay away from home: a free hotel room, airport-hotel transport and a
    USD 150 eCredit for every passenger.
3.2 Uncontrollable cancellation (for example weather) with an overnight stay: a free room from the contracted block and
    hotel transport ONLY for:
    - Gold, Platinum and Diamond members (and everyone on their booking);
    - passengers who need special assistance (SSR WCHR, WCHS, WCHC, BLND, DEAF, MEDA) — an accessible room where needed;
    - passengers travelling with a lap infant;
    - every passenger on a flight departing an EU or UK airport (EU261 / UK261 care obligation — see REG-EU-03).
    Other passengers away from home receive the **distressed-passenger rate** (about USD 89 a night, paid by the passenger)
    at a partner hotel. Local residents are not offered a hotel.
3.3 One room per booking (up to 3 passengers); larger parties receive a second room.
3.4 Unaccompanied minors are never placed in a hotel (see SSR-04).

## 4. Goodwill
Gold, Platinum and Diamond members whose trip is delayed overnight receive a USD 100 eCredit. Passengers whose booking was
split across flights receive a USD 100 eCredit.

## 5. Refunds
If Aeralis cancels a flight or makes a significant change and the passenger does not accept the alternative offered, the
passenger is entitled to a full refund to the original form of payment, including taxes, fees and optional services not
used — even on Basic Economy (see REG-US-04). A travel credit is offered only as an alternative the passenger may choose.
