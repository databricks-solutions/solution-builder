# Make-Good & Audience Deficiency Unit (ADU) Policy — SLS-POL-11

Owner: Ad Sales Operations. Applies to all guaranteed audience deals (Upfront Guaranteed, Scatter Guaranteed, Programmatic Guaranteed).

## §1 The guarantee
1.1 A guaranteed deal is measured on in-target impressions of its booked audience segment, across all its line items, over the flight.
1.2 The deal is under-delivered when in-target delivery at flight end is below 95% of the booked impressions. Once under-delivered, the full shortfall against 100% is owed as a make-good, valued at the deal's booked CPM (make-good liability).

## §2 Monitoring and notice
2.1 Ad Operations projects every live deal's delivery at flight end daily (delivered to date + recent run-rate x remaining days).
2.2 When projected delivery falls below 90% of the guarantee, Ad Sales must notify the advertiser's agency in writing within 2 business days, with a recovery plan (where the remaining impressions will run).
2.3 A recovery plan is approved by the Director of Yield & Pricing, and executed by Ad Operations (see AO-SOP-07).

## §3 Where make-goods may run
3.1 Make-goods run as ADUs (audience deficiency units) in inventory of EQUAL OR BETTER tier (Premium > Standard > RON) whose audience share for the booked segment is at least that of the under-delivering line.
3.2 ADUs may displace programmatic (PMP / open-auction) impressions. They may not displace other guaranteed deals.
3.3 ADUs normally run during the flight or within 30 days after flight end, unless the IO says otherwise.
3.4 Cash credits are a last resort and require approval by the Chief Revenue Officer.

## §4 Make-good history
Make-goods are logged per campaign with shortfall impressions, value, resolution (ADU or cash credit) and the inventory used. ADUs historically ran in Crest+ Run of Network, FAST Classic TV, ACN Daytime and Late Night — advertisers increasingly reject that as not equivalent (see IO-level terms).
