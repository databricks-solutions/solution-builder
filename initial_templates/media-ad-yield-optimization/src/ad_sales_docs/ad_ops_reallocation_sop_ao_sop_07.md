# Ad Operations SOP AO-SOP-07 — Rebalancing an under-delivering guaranteed deal

Owner: Ad Operations (Sam Patel, Ad Ops Lead).

## §1 Inputs
An approved recovery plan (Director of Yield & Pricing) naming: the campaign / IO, the impressions to move, the target products and daily caps, the displaced programmatic channel, and any floor-price change.

## §2 Steps
2.1 Create ADU line items on the target products under the same IO, targeted to the booked audience segment, flight = today to flight end, priority "Guaranteed - make-good" (above PMP and open auction).
2.2 Set daily caps so the ADUs deliver evenly over the remaining flight; never exceed the product's spare avails forecast.
2.3 Keep the under-delivering line running at its current run-rate — do not stop it.
2.4 Apply any floor-group change in the same change window, under its own ADOPS ticket, and log it in the floor-price rules history.
2.5 Send the agency the delivery confirmation within 1 business day, then report pacing weekly until flight end.

## §3 Timelines
Plans approved before 2pm PT go live the same day; otherwise the next business day.
