# Aurora Crest Media — 2026-27 Advertising Rate Card (RC-2026-27)

Owner: Ad Revenue & Yield (Jordan Ellis, SVP Ad Revenue). Effective for the 2026-27 broadcast year (upfront and scatter).
Applies to ACN (broadcast network), Crest+ (ad-supported streaming) and the Aurora Crest FAST channels.

## §1 How inventory is sold
1.1 Guaranteed audience deals (Upfront Guaranteed, Scatter Guaranteed, Programmatic Guaranteed) are sold on in-target impressions of a named audience segment and carry an audience guarantee (see Make-Good & ADU Policy SLS-POL-11).
1.2 Streaming and FAST inventory not used by guaranteed deals is sold programmatically: first to private-marketplace (PMP) deals, then to the open auction. Linear (ACN) inventory is not sold programmatically.
1.3 Every programmatic ad unit belongs to exactly ONE floor group. The floor group sets the minimum price the ad server accepts and which PMP deals can buy the unit.

## §3 Programmatic floor groups
| Floor group | Floor CPM | Who can buy it besides the open auction |
|---|---|---|
| PREMIUM-SPORTS | $30.00 | PMP-PREM-SPORTS (select sports buyers, floor $32) |

| STANDARD | $14.00 | PMP-STANDARD (floor $15) |
| RON-DEFAULT | $9.00 | PMP-RON-STREAM (fixed price $10.50, all DSPs) |

3.1 Premium inventory must never be sold programmatically below 50% of its rate card CPM. For Crest+ Live Sports that is $24.00.
3.2 Crest+ Live Sports is premium, scarce, sports-adjacent inventory and belongs to PREMIUM-SPORTS. It must never be in RON-DEFAULT, and it must never be bought by the run-of-network PMP PMP-RON-STREAM.
3.3 Discounts: upfront commitments may be discounted up to 22% off rate card; scatter up to 15%. Anything deeper needs SVP Ad Revenue approval.

## §4 Audience composition
In-target impressions = impressions x the product's audience share for the segment (audience mix table, refreshed monthly from ACR + first-party data). The highest Auto Intenders A25-54 reach in the portfolio is Crest+ Live Sports (30% of impressions), then ACN Live Sports (26%) and Crest+ Sports Studio (21%).
