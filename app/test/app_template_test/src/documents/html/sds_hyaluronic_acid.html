Safety Data Sheet — Hyaluronic Acid Powder

Sodium hyaluronate, low molecular weight · Cosmetic grade

1. Identification

Product name: Hyaluronic Acid Powder
Chemical: Sodium hyaluronate (low molecular weight)
Intended use: Cosmetic humectant — actives in serums and creams.
Supplier: Acme Botanicals SARL, Lille, France.

2. Hazard identification

GHS classification: not hazardous. May cause mild eye irritation on direct contact with dry powder. No skin sensitization or respiratory hazard at intended-use concentrations (0.1-1.5% in finished cosmetic).

3. Composition

Pure sodium hyaluronate ≥ 98%. Trace excipients < 2% (sorbitol, deionized-water residue from drying).

4. First aid measures

  • Eye: rinse with running water for 15 minutes; consult physician if irritation persists.
  • Skin: wash with soap and water; if irritation persists, consult physician.
  • Ingestion: rinse mouth; no further action required at intended-use levels.

5. Storage

Cool, dry storage away from direct sunlight. Sealed-container shelf life is 36 months from manufacture. Re-test required after that date before further use.

6. Regulatory

Compliant with EU Cosmetics Regulation 1223/2009 Annex II/III; INCI name Sodium Hyaluronate. Not subject to REACH restriction at intended-use concentrations.
