Sustainability Packaging Roadmap 2026-2028

Sustainability & Packaging · Issue date 2026-01-20

Goal

Eliminate virgin plastic from primary packaging by 2028 and reach 100% recyclable secondary packaging by 2027. Year-1 target: 30% recycled content in Skincare bottles; 60% by year 2.

Materials transition

Suppliers

Provence Bottles (Skincare primary glass) and TruPack Cartons (secondary outer) have signed cooperative roadmaps. Selected vendor onboarding for compostable inserts is in progress.

Reporting

Annual progress published in the LuxeBeauty Sustainability Report. Internal quarterly review with the Sustainability Steering Committee.
