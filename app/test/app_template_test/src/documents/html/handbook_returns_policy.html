Returns & Refunds Policy

LuxeBeauty Customer Care Handbook · Version 2025-11

Standard returns

LuxeBeauty accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for any reason. Customer pays return postage; refund is processed to the original payment method within 5 business days of receipt and inspection. Items must be in original packaging with the safety seal intact.

Defective product

For returns flagged as defective, the customer is eligible for a refund within 90 days of delivery. LuxeBeauty issues a prepaid postage label and processes a full refund plus a 10% goodwill credit on the next purchase. Tier-A customers (Gold loyalty AND premium-tagged) receive a full refund, a 20% goodwill credit, and a handwritten apology card.

Bulk returns and lot-level events

If Operations identifies an issue that warrants outreach to a specific cohort of customers, the Customer Care team notifies the affected customers within 48 hours of the disposition decision. Refunds and free replacements are issued automatically; no customer action is required.
