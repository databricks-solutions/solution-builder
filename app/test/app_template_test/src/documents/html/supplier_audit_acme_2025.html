Supplier Audit Report — Acme Botanicals SARL

Audit period: 2025 · Conducted by LuxeBeauty QA · Confidential

Supplier

Acme Botanicals SARL is LuxeBeauty's primary supplier for cosmetic-grade hyaluronic acid powder and botanical extracts feeding the Skincare line. The supplier has been on the qualified vendor list since 2018.

Audit scope

On 2025-09-14, LuxeBeauty QA visited Acme's Lille production facility. The audit covered the COA issuance process, mass-balance reconciliation between receipt and dispatch, lot-traceability from raw material to finished pack, and COA archival.

Findings

  • COA system: PASS. All batches reconcile to vendor source documents; certificates carry signed approvals.
  • Mass-balance: PASS. Less than 0.4% drift across the audited window of 240 batches.
  • Traceability: PASS. Lot codes carry through to LuxeBeauty's receiving system intact.
  • Archival: minor finding. Suggest extending COA retention from 5y to 7y to align with our internal practice and the EU Cosmetics Regulation reference period.

Conclusion

Acme Botanicals remains approved as the primary hyaluronic acid supplier for LuxeBeauty Skincare. Re-audit scheduled for 2026-Q3.
