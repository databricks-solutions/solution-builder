Holiday 2025 Campaign Brief — Gift Sets & Pre-Loyalty Push

Marketing · Issued 2025-09-15 · Campaign window: 2025-11-15 to 2025-12-31

Objective

Drive year-end revenue with a curated gift-set assortment and convert mid-funnel prospects into Loyalty members ahead of the New-Year free-shipping window. Target: +18% YoY gross revenue in the campaign window; +7% YoY new Loyalty signups.

Hero SKUs

The hero set is the Skincare Discovery Trio (3-piece travel size). Secondary push around Makeup palettes and the Fragrance dual-pack. Note: hero SKUs do not include the Hydrating Serum 30ml line — that line ships in the spring refresh campaign instead.

Channels

  • Paid social: Instagram + TikTok, 60% of spend.
  • Programmatic display: 25% of spend, mostly EU + US tier-1 metros.
  • Influencer partnerships: 10 mid-tier creators per market.
  • Email + SMS: 4-step nurture, with Loyalty-tier-specific subject lines.

Measurement

Daily ROAS dashboard owned by the Marketing Analytics team. Post-campaign retrospective scheduled 2026-01-20 with a written readout to the executive team.
