Production Incident Report — PIR-2026-0502

Facility: Lyon-France
Equipment: Homogenizer Unit HMG-03
Reporter: Marc Dupont, Production Supervisor
Date of incident: 2026-05-02
Affected SKUs: SKU-1001, SKU-1002, SKU-1003 (Skincare line — Hydrating Serum, Vitamin C Cream, HA Moisture Boost)
Affected lot: LOT-2026-0502
Units affected: ~5,000 units across the three SKUs

Summary

During the emulsification step on 2026-05-02 at the Lyon facility, Homogenizer Unit HMG-03 exhibited pressure-regulation drift. Gauge readings fluctuated between 2.1 and 2.8 bar over a 90-minute window while the normal operating range for this product family is 2.4 to 2.6 bar.

Root cause

Calibration drift in the pressure regulation valve PRV-03B. The valve had not been serviced since the last quarterly maintenance check (2026-02-15) and had drifted approximately 0.2 bar above nominal. Combined with the higher viscosity of the Skincare Serum / Cream / Treatment formulations on the line that day, the pressure fluctuation translated into inconsistent shear during emulsification.

QC assessment

QC inspector Sophie Martin reviewed a representative sample of 24 units across the three SKUs. The visual and texture inspection revealed:

"Some units may exhibit minor texture variations due to the pressure fluctuations during emulsification. The variations include slight phase separation visible in the bottle and a grainy or curdled texture when applied. This is a cosmetic variation only and does not affect product safety or efficacy. Recommend release for distribution with internal QC note."

Disposition

RELEASED for distribution per QC recommendation. Internal QC note flagged in lot file. No customer-facing communication issued at the time.

Corrective actions taken

  1. Valve PRV-03B replaced 2026-05-03.
  2. Calibration check schedule moved from quarterly to monthly for the Lyon Skincare line.
  3. Production logs reviewed back through 2026-03; no other affected lots identified.

Notes

Texture complaints on these three SKUs have been customer-visible since approximately 3 weeks after release (post-purchase use). Returns volume from LOT-2026-0502 currently trending 3x normal. Recommend review by VP Operations.
