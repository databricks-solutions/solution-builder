Ask the Assistant

Show me a 2x2 of CS label (premium / not_premium / unlabeled) against the model's prediction, with counts.

Three things should be true:

  1. There are model-found premiums — unlabeled customers the model flags premium. These are the hidden ones a WHERE premium_status='premium' filter can never return. That's the punchline.
  2. The model mostly agrees with CS where CS actually labeled — the diagonal (premium→premium, not_premium→not_premium) should dominate the off-diagonal.
  3. It is not flagging the majority of the unlabeled pool. If well over half of the unlabeled customers come back premium, the model has learned "say yes" — the labeled set is too small or too imbalanced to be useful. Say so: "the model predicts premium for most unlabeled customers — check class balance and recalibrate the threshold." A classifier that calls everyone premium tells the opposite of the story we're here to tell.
