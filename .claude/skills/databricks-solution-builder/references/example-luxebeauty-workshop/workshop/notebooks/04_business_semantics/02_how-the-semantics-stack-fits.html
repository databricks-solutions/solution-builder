How the semantics stack fits together

Your data already holds the answers — but the meaning is scattered across dashboards, SQL, spreadsheets and people's heads. Unity Catalog puts a governed business semantics layer directly on the data, so AI resolves the right definition instead of guessing from raw tables. Four layers, each solving one problem:

Raw data
Facts & events — orders, returns, production lots
Metric View
Makes the numbers right. Governed KPIs + dimensions, defined once — "return_rate = returns ÷ orders"
Pages & Domains
Make the language right. Authoritative definitions of terms & concepts (Pages), organized by business area (Domains) — "what counts as an anomaly"
Genie Ontology
Makes the context connected. Links your modeled definitions with knowledge inferred from dashboards, queries & agents; ranks sources by authority
Genie One
Makes it accessible to everyone. One front door: business users ask in plain English, grounded in the governed context — "why did returns spike in France?"

The strategy: model the head, infer the tail — hand-model your few most important KPIs and terms, and let the ontology infer the rest from what you've already built. We'll start that here with one metric view.
