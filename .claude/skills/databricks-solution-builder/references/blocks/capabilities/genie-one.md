# Genie One

**The enterprise AI coworker** that makes data and AI accessible to business users without technical expertise. (Formerly "Databricks One.")

## Pain

Business users can't self-serve. They ask data teams for every report, wait for IT to set up dashboards, never explore on their own. The platform UI is designed for engineers, not executives.

## Key Features

- **Personalized home** — recently accessed items, favorites, trending assets
- **Domain-based browsing** — content organized by business context, not technical structure
- **Unified search** — find dashboards, apps, Genie spaces across the platform
- **Chat interface** — conversational data exploration (beta)
- **Consumer access** — view-only users land directly in Databricks One

## Position

Any "how do business users access insights?" conversation. Perfect for non-technical stakeholders. "Your CEO lands here, sees dashboards, asks Genie questions — no training required."

## How It Works

- **Consumer access**: Business users with this entitlement land directly in Databricks One
- **Personalized discovery**: Home shows recently accessed, favorites, trending in your org
- **Domain-based navigation**: Browse by business domain (Sales, Finance, Operations) instead of technical catalogs
- **Search everything**: Dashboards, Genie spaces, apps — filter by type, owner, certification, date
- **View inline, edit in workspace**: Consumers view in Databricks One; power users jump to full editing

## Demo Tips

- Great for "who uses this?" — show the non-technical user experience
- Position as the "front door" for business users: "Your sales team starts here"
- Consumer licensing: "These users don't need full Databricks training"
- Show dashboards, Genie, and apps all surfacing in one place
- Governance: "They can only see what they're entitled to see"
