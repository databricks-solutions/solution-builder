# Databricks Marketplace

**Open data marketplace** for discovering and consuming third-party datasets, AI models, and solution accelerators.

## Pain

Finding external data means vendor portals, contract negotiations, ETL pipelines. By the time you get it, the business question has moved on. Third-party data lives in silos, disconnected from your lakehouse.

## Key Features

- **One-click access** — datasets appear directly in Unity Catalog
- **2,200+ listings** — datasets, AI models, notebooks, solution accelerators
- **Delta Sharing powered** — zero-copy, no data movement
- **Public + private exchanges** — open listings or invite-only for partners
- **Cross-platform** — access from Databricks, Power BI, pandas, Spark

## Position

Enriching internal data with external sources. "Need weather data? Geolocation? Financial benchmarks? One click and it's in your catalog."

## How It Works

- **Browse**: 2,200+ listings from 230+ providers — datasets, models, accelerators
- **Subscribe with one click**: Accept terms, data appears as a catalog in your workspace
- **No data movement**: Delta Sharing delivers live data without copying — always current, no ETL
- **Private exchanges**: Invite-only exchanges for B2B sharing with specific organizations
- **List your own**: Become a provider — share public datasets or monetize commercial offerings

## Demo Tips

- Great for "where does external data come from?" questions
- Instant enrichment: "subscribe and it's in your catalog in seconds"
- Breadth: weather, geolocation, financial, ESG, IoT datasets
- Governance: "same UC permissions as your internal data"
