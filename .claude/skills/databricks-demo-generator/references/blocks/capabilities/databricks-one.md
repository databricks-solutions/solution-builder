# Databricks One

**Simplified interface** that makes data and AI accessible to business users without technical expertise.

## Pain

Business users can't self-serve. They need to ask data teams for every report, wait for IT to set up dashboards, and never truly explore data on their own. The traditional data platform UI is designed for engineers, not executives.

## Key Features

- **Personalized home** - recently accessed items, favorites, trending assets
- **Domain-based browsing** - content organized by business context, not technical structure
- **Unified search** - find dashboards, apps, Genie spaces across the entire platform
- **Chat interface** - conversational data exploration (beta)
- **Consumer access** - view-only users land directly in Databricks One

## Position

Any "how do business users access insights?" conversation. Perfect for executives and non-technical stakeholders who need data without learning SQL or navigating notebooks. "Your CEO lands here, sees dashboards, asks Genie questions — no training required."

## How It Works

- **Users get assigned Consumer access**: Business users with this entitlement land directly in Databricks One
- **Personalized discovery**: Home page shows recently accessed items, favorites, and what's trending in your org
- **Domain-based navigation**: Browse by business domain (Sales, Finance, Operations) instead of technical catalogs
- **Search everything**: Find dashboards, Genie spaces, apps — filter by type, owner, certification, date
- **View inline, edit in workspace**: Consumers view assets in Databricks One; power users can jump to full editing

## Demo Tips

- Great for the "who uses this?" question — show the non-technical user experience
- Position as the "front door" for business users: "Your sales team starts here"
- Mention consumer licensing: "These users don't need full Databricks training"
- Show how dashboards, Genie, and apps all surface in one place
- Emphasize governance: "They can only see what they're entitled to see"
