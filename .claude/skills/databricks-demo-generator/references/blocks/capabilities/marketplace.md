# Databricks Marketplace

**Open data marketplace** for discovering and consuming third-party datasets, AI models, and solution accelerators.

## Pain

Finding external data means navigating vendor portals, negotiating contracts, and building ETL pipelines. By the time you get the data, the business question has moved on. Third-party data lives in silos, disconnected from your lakehouse.

## Key Features

- **One-click access** - datasets appear directly in your Unity Catalog
- **2,200+ listings** - datasets, AI models, notebooks, solution accelerators
- **Delta Sharing powered** - zero-copy, no data movement
- **Public + private exchanges** - open listings or invite-only for partners
- **Cross-platform** - consumers can access from Databricks, Power BI, pandas, Spark

## Position

Any conversation about enriching internal data with external sources. "Need weather data? Geolocation? Financial benchmarks? One click and it's in your catalog."

## How It Works

- **Browse the marketplace**: Search 2,200+ listings from 230+ providers — datasets, models, accelerators
- **Subscribe with one click**: Select a listing, accept terms — the data appears as a catalog in your workspace
- **No data movement**: Delta Sharing delivers live data without copying — always current, no ETL
- **Private exchanges for partners**: Create invite-only exchanges for B2B data sharing with specific organizations
- **List your own data products**: Become a provider — share public datasets or monetize commercial offerings
